Clara's heart raced as she peered deeper into the shadows of the corridor, the flickering lantern casting long, spindly shadows that crawled along the damp stone walls like fingers reaching out. The air was thick with the scent of mildew, mingling with the earthy aroma of wet soil, a reminder of the greenhouse they had just escaped. She felt the weight of the parlor key against her wrist, the red ribbon a stark contrast against the pallor of her skin, a tether to something both familiar and unsettling.

Jun shifted beside her, the sound of his wet coat rustling like a distant storm. "We shouldn't be here," he whispered, his voice trembling as if it were caught between panic and disbelief. He clutched the lantern tighter, its glow sputtering again, dimming to an anxious flicker. Clara could see the fear etched into his features, the way his brow furrowed, and she felt an instinctive urge to calm him. But the words caught in her throat, tangled with her own rising dread.

“Just a little further,” she said, more sharply than intended, the echo of her voice bouncing off the stones, ricocheting back to her with a weight she hadn’t anticipated. She bit her lip, the taste of iron mingling with the dampness of the air, as she forced herself to step forward, each footfall careful against the slick floor. The muddy prints ahead of them were a jarring contrast to the cold stone, tracking away into the darkness, leading them deeper into the unknown.
