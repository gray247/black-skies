Clara could feel her heart hammering in her chest, a frantic rhythm that drowned out the distant echoes of the house. The chain on the door rattled again, taut and insistent, as if someone—or something—was testing it from the other side. Clara’s fingers instinctively brushed the red ribbon around her wrist, the parlor key dangling like a promise or a threat, she couldn’t quite tell which.

She turned her gaze to Jun, seeing the worry etched into his features, the way his brow furrowed in the lantern’s soft light. “It’s just the wind.”
