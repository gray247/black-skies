"Do you think anyone is up there?" Jun's voice broke through the silence, a thin thread of fear woven into his words. Clara shook her head, but the motion felt hollow, as if she were trying to convince herself more than him.

"I don’t know," she replied, her voice sharper than intended. The words hung between them, heavy with unspoken fears. It felt wrong, as if the ground were breathing, alive with secrets that were not meant to be unearthed.
