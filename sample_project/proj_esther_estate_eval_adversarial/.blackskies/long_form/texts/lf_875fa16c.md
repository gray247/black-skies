He cast a glance back toward the door they had just forced open, the chain rattling ominously as if it were alive, testing its bonds from the other side.

“Just a little further,” she urged, her tone sharper than she intended, laced with a bravado she didn’t feel. Clara’s gaze darted down the corridor, straining to pierce the darkness that felt almost tangible. The red ribbon around her wrist pulled at her skin, the parlor key a constant reminder of the secrets buried within the walls. She couldn’t remember when she had tied it tighter, but it felt crucial now, a talisman against the encroaching dread.
