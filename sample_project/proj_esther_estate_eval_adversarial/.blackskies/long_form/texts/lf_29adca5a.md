The room inside was cloaked in shadows, the only light a dim halo from the lantern that flickered like a dying star. Clara stepped in, her breath hitching as the familiar scent of dust and old wood enveloped her, mingling with something sweet and decayed. It was a smell that spoke of lost innocence and forgotten dreams, and it made her stomach churn.

“Nothing here,” Jun said, his voice a whisper, yet it echoed in the vast emptiness. He took a step in behind her, and the floorboards groaned as if protesting their intrusion. “Just… just dust.”
