She could almost feel the weight of someone testing it, a presence that lurked just out of reach. “Do you think someone’s…?” she started, but the words fell away, the potential horror hanging heavy between them.

Jun shook his head, water streaking down his face, heavy with the weight of uncertainty. “We can’t just stand here. Let’s see if we can open it.” He reached for the door, but Clara grabbed his arm, a sharpness in her grip that surprised her. “Wait. What if it’s... what if it’s not safe?”
