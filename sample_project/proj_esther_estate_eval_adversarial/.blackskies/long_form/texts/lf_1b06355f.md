Clara’s fingers brushed the damp wall as she edged forward, the chill of the plaster seeping into her skin, grounding her amid the unsettling uncertainty. The light from Jun’s lantern flickered weakly, casting erratic shadows that danced like specters along the corridor, each flicker deepening the hollows of her anxiety. She could feel the weight of the parlor key against her wrist, the red ribbon a taut reminder of her choices, yet she couldn’t recall tightening it. Was it the rain that made her shiver, or something more sinister lurking just beyond the chained door?

“Maybe we should go back,” Jun suggested, his voice barely more than a breath. His coat was still soaked from the downpour in the greenhouse, droplets clinging to the fabric like the remnants of their earlier courage. The dimness swallowed his features, but she sensed his eyes darting nervously, searching for threats in the shadows.

“Back? We can’t go back now. Not after…” Clara’s words trailed off, the faint echo of a child’s cough still ringing in her ears, haunting and distant. It was a sound she wished she could dismiss, but it coiled around her thoughts, tightening with each passing moment. “We need to find out what’s happening.”

The chain on the door rattled again, a sound that felt all too deliberate, as if someone on the other side were testing their resolve. Clara’s heart raced, each pulse a drumbeat of dread. “Did you hear that?” she asked, her voice sharper than intended. Jun nodded, his expression tightening as the shadows loomed larger, almost as if they were closing in around them.
