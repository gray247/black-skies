“I’m sure,” Clara replied, though the uncertainty gnawed at her insides. In that moment, she couldn’t shake the feeling that the ribbon was a lifeline, anchoring her to something real amid the encroaching shadows.

With trembling hands, she fitted the key into the lock, its cold metal fitting snugly as she turned it. Clara swallowed hard, glancing at Jun, who held his breath, his eyes wide in anticipation.

“What if it’s just the wind?” he suggested, but the quiver in his voice betrayed his own doubts.
