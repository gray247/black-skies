Jun’s fingers tightened around the cracked brass handle, the metal cool and slick from the damp of his coat, which clung to him like a second skin, heavy with the weight of the greenhouse rain. Clara walked beside him, her breath coming in sharp bursts, each inhale laced with the musty scent of old wood and mildew. The air felt thick, almost electric, as if charged with secrets waiting to spill.

The sound ricocheted off the walls, amplifying the oppressive silence that followed, a silence that felt too heavy, too watchful.
