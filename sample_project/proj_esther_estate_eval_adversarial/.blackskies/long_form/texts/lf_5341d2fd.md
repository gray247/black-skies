Jun shifted beside her, the lantern sputtering again, casting long, jittery shadows that danced along the floorboards like restless spirits.

“Clara,” he said, his voice a whisper, barely escaping the confines of their dim sanctuary. The lantern flickered, and she caught the glint of worry etched on his face, a worry that mirrored her own. “Maybe we should go back?”

“No,” she breathed, the word sharp and resolute, though inside she felt the tension coil tighter in her chest. The key on her wrist—its ribbon frayed yet stubbornly tied—seemed to pulse with her heartbeat, urging her onward. “We have to see.”

The door loomed before her, the chipped paint peeling back in places like the layers of a secret kept too long. She steeled herself, her heart racing, and turned the key in the lock. It clicked, an echoing sound in the silence, but the door didn’t budge. She frowned, the knot in her stomach tightening further as she twisted the key again, frustration prickling at the edges of her resolve.

Jun shifted beside her, his breath catching. “Maybe it’s just stuck? Or locked from the inside?”

“Or maybe…” Clara’s voice trailed off as the sound of the chain rattling above them sliced through the stillness, a harsh reminder that they were not alone here. She glanced up, instinctively looking for the source, her mind racing. “Did you hear that?”

He nodded, a ghost of uncertainty crossing his features. “I thought it was just… the house,” he said, but his words lacked conviction. The very walls seemed to breathe, settling and shifting, the house alive with history and whispers, secrets hidden in the dark.

Clara pressed her palm against the door, the wood cool beneath her skin, and she felt an inexplicable pull—a longing to know what lay beyond. “Jun, we can’t just stand here. If there’s something… someone in there, we need to find out.”

He hesitated, glancing back down the corridor, where shadows clung to the corners, murmuring of forgotten things. “What if it’s dangerous?”

“It’s already dangerous,” Clara shot back, her voice sharper than intended, the tension sparking between them. The weight of the moment felt too heavy, too charged, and she could see the flicker of fear in his eyes, a fear that mirrored her own.
