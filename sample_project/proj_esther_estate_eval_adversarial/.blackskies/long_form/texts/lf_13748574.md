Each step echoed softly against the walls, a quiet reminder of their presence in a house that felt far too alive, too aware.

“Do you hear that?” Jun’s voice was a tense whisper, barely rising above the soft patter of water dripping from Clara’s coat pocket, where the chipped ceramic fox nestled against her fingers. She could feel the coolness of the clay, and the thought of its unyielding gaze sent a shiver racing down her spine.

“What?” she snapped, her words sharper than intended, betraying the fear that flickered beneath her bravado. She tightened the red ribbon around her wrist, the parlor key dangling slightly, a small weight tethering her to some semblance of control.
