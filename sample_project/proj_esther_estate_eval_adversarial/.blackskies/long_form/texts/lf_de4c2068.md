She could see the glistening footprints in the mud ahead, their origins a mystery that clawed at her resolve. “We should go back,” Jun whispered, his voice trembling, an echo of the fear that had begun to wrap around her own heart like a noose.

“No,” Clara snapped, more sharply than she intended. The edges of her frustration were fraying, unraveling like the damp fabric of her coat. “We can’t.
