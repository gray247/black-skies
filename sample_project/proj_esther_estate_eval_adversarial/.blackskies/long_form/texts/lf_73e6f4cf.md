“A cough, Jun. A child’s cough,” she interrupted, her heart racing with a mixture of dread and urgency. “I swear I heard it.”

He shook his head, his uncertainty a palpable weight between them. “There’s no child here,” he said, but his voice lacked conviction, as if he too felt the weight of the nursery’s history pressing down on them.
