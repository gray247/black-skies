“Maybe it was just the wind,” he offered, but even he sounded unconvinced. The chain rattled softly from the service corridor beyond, a sound that sent a chill coursing through her. The moment stretched, a taut thread straining against their uncertainty. She glanced sideways at Jun, who nodded slightly, his determination rekindling her own. “Three.”
