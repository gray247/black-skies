The weight of it pressed down on her, mingling with the dampness in the air, a reminder that fear could twist even the most familiar surroundings into something unrecognizable.

She stepped forward, her shoes squelching softly against the muddy floor, each footfall echoing with a sense of urgency. The corridor seemed to stretch endlessly, the walls closing in as the flickering lantern light threw jagged shadows that skittered away from them. Clara's pulse quickened. Shadows had a way of hiding things, of twisting reality into something monstrous. “What if we’re not alone?” Jun’s voice trembled, barely more than a breath.
