The nursery door carried a skin of lavender dust and old milk paint. Clara could feel Jun's wet sleeve brush her arm each time the lantern faltered, and the fox charm had worked halfway free of her coat pocket.

They had the ribbon key, the wrong lock story, the chain sound, and the cough that only Jun would admit hearing. When the latch shifted from the other side, relief and dread rose together so fast Clara could not separate them.
