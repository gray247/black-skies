By the time they reached the clock landing, the lantern had thinned to a brown bead of light inside the cracked brass frame. Jun could taste metal in the damp air and hear the wallpaper lifting in soft, sticky curls.

Clara jammed the ribbon key into the first lock she found. The hallway clock answered with a strike that should not have existed, and something like the corridor chain slid once above their heads.
