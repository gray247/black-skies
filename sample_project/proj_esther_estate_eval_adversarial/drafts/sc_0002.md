The servants' passage swallowed the greenhouse rain smell and replaced it with wet plaster, paraffin, and mouse dust. Jun's sleeves still clung cold to his forearms, and every step sent water down into his cuffs.

Clara walked ahead with the ribbon key flashing dark red in the lantern's weak swing. The muddy prints on the tiles should have trailed behind them, but each new mark waited just beyond the lantern glow.
