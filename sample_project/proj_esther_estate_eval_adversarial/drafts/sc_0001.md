Rain glazed the greenhouse panes until every row of glass looked painted over. Clara kept one hand on the red ribbon at her wrist and the other on the swollen latch while Jun angled the brass lantern away from the leaking roof.

The bench of seed trays had collapsed into wet soil and shards. Somewhere above them, the house knocked once, as if answering the hiss of the storm.
