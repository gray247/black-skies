A stray cat darted past, its fur matted and dark against the dim light filtering from a cracked window above. Lena leaned against the cool brick wall, her heart racing in rhythm with the distant chime of a clock striking eight. She could feel the pulse of the city around her, the thrum of life echoing in the shadows.

Her fingers grazed the rough surface of the wall, grounding her in the moment, reminding her that she was still here, still breathing. A faint rustle made her glance back down the alley, where the shadows seemed to stretch and contort, shapes that danced just beyond the edge of her vision. She swallowed hard, the metallic taste of anxiety lingering on her tongue.

“Are you sure about this?” a voice broke through her thoughts, sharp as the chill in the air. It was Marcus, his brow furrowed under the brim of his battered cap. He shifted from one foot to the other, his hands tucked deep in his pockets. “We could just walk away, you know.”
