The wind whistled through the narrow alley, a sharp breath of winter that cut against Clara's cheeks. She pulled her scarf tighter, the wool coarse against her skin, but it offered a small comfort as she continued down the cobblestone path. Each step echoed with a hollow thud, the sound swallowed by the looming shadows of the buildings that leaned in like conspirators.

She glanced up, her breath fogging the air, and caught sight of the moon, pale and watchful, casting a silvery glow that danced across the uneven stones. The quiet of the night was punctuated only by the distant hum of laughter from a tavern, mingling with the clinking of glasses, a reminder of warmth just beyond reach. Clara’s stomach twisted with a mix of hunger and longing, but she pressed on, the urgency in her stride betraying her calm facade.

A sound—a soft scuffle—caused her to halt, heart racing. She turned, straining to pierce the darkness. A figure shifted in the shadows, obscured but undeniably present. “Who's there?” she called, her voice steadier than she felt, echoing off the damp walls. The figure hesitated, then stepped into the moonlight, revealing a young man with tousled hair and eyes that glinted with mischief.

“Just a traveler,” he said, his tone light, though Clara could sense the tension beneath. “Looking for a way through this maze.” His hands were shoved deep in his pockets, and he shifted his weight, as if ready to bolt at the slightest hint of danger.

“Maze or not, you picked a strange time to wander,” Clara replied, her own unease creeping back. She could feel the pulse of the night, the weight of silence pressing in around them, and she wondered if he was as lost as he seemed or if there was something darker lurking behind those playful eyes.
