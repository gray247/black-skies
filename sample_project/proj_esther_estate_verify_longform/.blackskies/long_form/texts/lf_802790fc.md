Clara huddled beneath the awning of a small café, its neon sign flickering weakly in the downpour. The scent of wet asphalt mingled with the rich aroma of brewing coffee, drawing her in despite the chaos outside. She could see droplets racing each other down the glass, each one a tiny vessel of decision, yet none reaching the bottom before merging into the next.

“Another five minutes, and I’m going to drown out here,” she muttered to herself, her voice barely audible over the storm. The thought of stepping into the rain sent a shiver through her, not from the cold, but from the weight of what she was about to do. She wrapped her arms around herself, fingers brushing against the fabric of her coat, feeling the heartbeat of her own nerves thrumming beneath the surface.
