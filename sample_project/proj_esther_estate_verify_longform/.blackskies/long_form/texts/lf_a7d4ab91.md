Rain drummed against the tin roof, a relentless rhythm that filled the small café with a sense of cozy urgency. The scent of freshly ground coffee mingled with the sharpness of wet earth as the door swung open, letting in a gust of chilly air. A shiver danced down Elara's spine as she glanced up from her worn notebook, half-filled with sketches of faces and notes scrawled in hurried ink. The bell above the door jingled, a cheerful sound that felt out of place against the backdrop of the storm.

“Just in time for the downpour,” a voice chimed from the counter. It was Marla, her hair a wild halo of curls that seemed to defy the dampness outside. She wiped her hands on her apron, her smile warm as she poured another cup of coffee. “You want the usual?”

Elara nodded, her fingers tapping absently against the notebook. The pages felt rough and familiar beneath her touch, a refuge from the chaos that thrummed outside. “Yeah, please. Black. No sugar.” She watched as Marla expertly balanced the cup on a saucer, the steam curling up like a dancer reaching for the sky.
