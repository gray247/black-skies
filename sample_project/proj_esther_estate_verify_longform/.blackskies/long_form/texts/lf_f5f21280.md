The door creaked open, the sound echoing softly in the dim light of the hallway. Eliza hesitated, her breath caught in her throat as the faint scent of musty paper wafted toward her, a nostalgic blend of old books and dust that stirred memories she wasn’t quite ready to face. She stepped inside, the wooden floorboards groaning under her weight, a subtle reminder of the years that had settled here like the dust on the shelves.

The room was a patchwork of shadows and light, the sun filtering through the half-drawn curtains, casting stripes across the floor that danced with the dust motes swirling in the air. Eliza's fingers grazed the spines of the books lining the wall, their titles faded and forgotten, each one a silent witness to the lives lived within these walls. She closed her eyes for a moment, letting the silence wrap around her like an old quilt, comforting yet suffocating.

“Are you coming, or are you going to stand there all day?” a voice broke the stillness, sharp and tinged with impatience. It was Sam, leaning casually against the doorframe, arms crossed, his brow arched in that infuriating way that always made her want to smile and scowl simultaneously.

“Right, sorry,” she replied, forcing herself to move deeper into the room. The air was thick with unspoken words, and she could feel the weight of their shared history pressing down on her. “I just… I didn’t expect it to feel like this.”

“Like what?” Sam stepped in, the light catching the edges of his tousled hair, making him look almost ethereal, more of a ghost than a man. “Haunted?”
