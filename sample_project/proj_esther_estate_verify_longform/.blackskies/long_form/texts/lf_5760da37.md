The wind whipped through the narrow alley, carrying with it the scent of rain-soaked cobblestones and the faint trace of roasting chestnuts from a nearby vendor. Elara pulled her cloak tighter around her shoulders, the fabric damp against her skin, as she hurried past the flickering gas lamps that cast trembling shadows against the walls. Each step echoed softly, a muted rhythm amidst the distant hum of the market just beyond the archway. Voices mingled, laughter punctuating the air, a stark contrast to the solitude she felt enveloped in.

“Are you certain this is the right place?” she muttered under her breath, glancing at the crumbling stone facade looming ahead. The door was ajar, a sliver of warm light spilling out into the night. She hesitated, the pulse of her heart quickening, a drumbeat of apprehension.

“Just a moment longer,” she whispered to herself, the words catching in her throat. Memories flooded her mind—her mother’s gentle hands brushing back her hair, the stories of bravery and adventure that had once filled their small home. But this was no fairy tale; the weight of reality pressed down on her. The flicker of a figure just inside the doorway made her freeze.

“Are you coming in or just going to stand there like a ghost?” The voice was low, teasing, but there was an edge to it, a warning laced beneath the humor.

Elara swallowed hard, her breath hitching as she stepped forward, crossing the threshold into the dimly lit room.
