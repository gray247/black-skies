The rain fell in heavy sheets, drumming against the roof with a relentless rhythm, a symphony of nature that drowned out the world outside. Inside the small café, warm light spilled from brass fixtures, casting a soft glow that hugged the wooden tables and the worn leather chairs. The smell of roasted coffee beans mingled with the sweet undertones of freshly baked pastries, creating a cozy cocoon where the outside chill felt a million miles away.

Elena cradled a steaming cup in her hands, the heat radiating through the porcelain and warming her fingers. She watched the rain cascade down the window, rivulets racing each other to the sill. Each drop carried away a fragment of her thoughts, but the rest swirled in her mind like the milk swirling into her coffee. She took a sip, savoring the bitter notes catching in her throat, grounding her in the moment.

A bell jingled above the door, and she turned to see a figure shake off the rain, droplets dancing off their shoulders. It was Marco, his dark hair plastered to his forehead, a crooked smile breaking through the gloom. “You look like you’re plotting world domination,” he said, sliding into the chair opposite her, his voice warm and teasing, cutting through her introspection.
