The bell above the café door jingled, a cheerful sound that clashed with the gray drizzle painting the streets outside. Emily pushed her way in, shaking droplets from her umbrella, the air inside warm and filled with the rich aroma of freshly ground coffee. She inhaled deeply, the scent wrapping around her like a familiar embrace. The wooden floor creaked underfoot as she made her way to the counter, her shoes leaving faint damp prints.

“Morning, Em.” The barista’s voice cut through the low hum of conversation, a friendly lilt that made her smile. Sam was already pouring an espresso, the dark liquid swirling and bubbling as it filled the cup.

“Morning, Sam,” she replied, her voice slightly muffled by the wool scarf wrapped around her neck. She leaned against the counter, watching him work, the way he focused, lost in the rhythm of his craft. A flicker of envy shot through her—his ease, his certainty. She wished she could capture that same flow in her life, where every day felt like a dance rather than a stumble.

“Your usual?” he asked, glancing up with a quick grin.

“Yeah, please.” She fished out her wallet, fingers brushing against the worn leather. As he prepared her drink, she let her gaze wander around the café. The walls were adorned with local art, each piece telling a story of its own, while small tables held clusters of patrons, some engrossed in conversation, others lost in the pages of books. A couple in the corner laughed, their voices rising and falling like a melody, while a man at the window stared out, a frown etched on his brow, perhaps wrestling with his own storm.

“Here you go.” Sam slid the cup toward her, steam rising in lazy curls. She wrapped her fingers around it, feeling the warmth seep into her skin, grounding her in the moment.
