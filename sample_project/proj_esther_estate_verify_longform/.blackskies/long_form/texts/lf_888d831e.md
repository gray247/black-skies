The air was thick with the scent of damp earth, clinging to her skin as Elara navigated through the crooked alleys of Ashwood. Each step sent a soft squelch beneath her boots, a reminder of the rain that had pelted the cobblestones just hours before. The shadows of the buildings loomed like ancient sentinels, their edges softened by the lingering mist, and Elara pulled her cloak tighter around her shoulders, feeling the wool brush against her arms, a small comfort against the chill.

Ahead, the flicker of a lantern caught her eye, its glow spilling warmth onto the stones. She hesitated, the familiar tug of uncertainty knotting in her stomach. Voices drifted from the nearby tavern, laughter mingling with the clatter of tankards. A part of her longed for the camaraderie, the simple joy of sharing a drink with old friends. But another part—the sharper, more cautious side—urged her to keep moving, to remain hidden.

“Oi, Elara!” A voice broke through her thoughts, rough yet familiar. It was Tamsin, leaning against the tavern doorframe, a half-smile playing on his lips. “You look like a ghost. Come on, join us. We got news.”

Her pulse quickened at the invitation, an insistent pull in the pit of her stomach. She stepped closer, the warmth of the lantern brushing against her face, and caught the scent of spiced mead wafting from within. “What news?” she asked, her curiosity battling the instinct to retreat.

Tamsin gestured her forward, his eyes glinting with mischief. “Something big. They’re saying the council’s got a plan, and it involves—” He leaned closer, lowering his voice as if the shadows themselves might listen. “Involves you.”
