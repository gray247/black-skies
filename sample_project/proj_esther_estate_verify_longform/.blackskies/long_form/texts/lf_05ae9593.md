Elara snorted despite herself, the sound breaking through the tension. “You’ve been reading too many fairy tales. Shadows don’t whisper; they just hide.” Yet, she couldn’t shake the feeling that there was truth woven into his words. The alley around them seemed to lean closer, the darkness gathering like a shroud, eager to absorb their fears.

“But what if they do?” Jace pressed, a hint of mischief dancing in his eyes. “What if they’re just waiting for someone brave enough to ask?”

Elara took a breath, steadying herself. “Okay, left it is.” She stepped forward again, her pulse thrumming in her ears, drowning out the sound of the wind.
