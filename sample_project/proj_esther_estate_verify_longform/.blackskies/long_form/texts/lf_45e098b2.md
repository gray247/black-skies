The wind whipped through the narrow streets of Eldenborough, carrying with it the sharp scent of rain-soaked earth and the distant tang of salt from the coast. A soft drizzle had begun to fall, each droplet tapping against the cobblestones like a gentle drummer setting a rhythm for the evening. Elara pulled her cloak tighter around her shoulders, the fabric heavy with the weight of the coming night, and quickened her pace, her boots splashing through puddles that shimmered like broken glass in the fading light.

Her breath came in soft puffs, mingling with the chill that wrapped around her. A flicker of movement caught her eye—a shadow darting between the alleyways, gone before she could fully register it. Heart racing, she glanced over her shoulder. Just a cat, she told herself, forcing a smile as her pulse slowed. But the unease lingered. The streets felt too quiet, as if the very stones were holding their breath.

As she turned the corner, the local tavern came into view, its windows glowing with warm light and laughter spilling forth like an inviting embrace. She hesitated, the familiar sounds of clinking glasses and boisterous shouts pulling at her reluctance. Inside, she’d find faces she knew, stories shared over pints of ale. But tonight, her thoughts spiraled like the steam rising from the gutters, tangled with the weight of her secret.

“Hey! Elara!” A voice broke through the haze, and she turned to see Finn leaning against the tavern doorframe, his carefree grin cutting through her apprehension. “You’ve been avoiding us. Come on in! It’s a proper night for tales.”

“Just... needed some air,” she replied, forcing a smile despite the tension coiling in her stomach. “You know how it gets when everyone’s in a mood.”
