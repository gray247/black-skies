A gust of cool air swept through the narrow hallway, sending a shiver down her spine. She hesitated at the threshold, the weight of the world pressing against her chest, but the soft glow of candlelight flickering in the main room beckoned her forward.

“Claire?” The voice was low, almost a whisper, yet it sliced through the stillness like a knife. She stepped inside, the floorboards groaning beneath her feet as she crossed the threshold into the small, dimly lit space. The room was alive with shadows, dancing along the walls, and she could barely make out the figure seated near the hearth.

“Yeah, it’s me,” she replied, her voice steadier than she felt. The warmth of the fire washed over her like a wave, its crackling sound pulling her deeper into the room. She could see him now—Eli, his face half-illuminated by the flickering flames. Lines of worry etched into his forehead, making him look older than his years.

“What took you so long?” He leaned forward, elbows resting on his knees, a frown tugging at the corners of his mouth.

“Just… needed to think.” She brushed a hand through her hair, the damp strands sticking to her forehead. A bead of anxiety rolled in her stomach. “Things are getting complicated.”
