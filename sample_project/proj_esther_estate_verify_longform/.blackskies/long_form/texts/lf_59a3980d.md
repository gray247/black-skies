“I’m fine,” Clara lied, her voice wavering as she avoided Lila’s gaze. She felt the weight of the mug in her hands, a distraction from the tension coiling in her stomach. “Just…thinking.”

“About?” The single word hung in the air, heavy and expectant, a thread of concern weaving through it. Lila leaned against the table, arms crossed, her stance both casual and probing.

Clara hesitated, the storm outside echoing the turmoil within. “Everything,” she finally admitted, her voice barely a whisper. “It’s just…hard to see what’s next when the world feels like this.” She gestured vaguely toward the window, her heart pounding as she spoke.

“Yeah, I get that.” Lila’s expression softened, and for a moment, the tension in the air dissipated like the steam from the mug. “But you can’t stay cooped up forever. You have to make a choice, Clara. You can’t just wait for the storm to pass.”
