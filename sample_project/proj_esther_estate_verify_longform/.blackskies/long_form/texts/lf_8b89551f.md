The forest enveloped Teresa, each breath filling her lungs with the rich, loamy scent of damp earth and decaying leaves. The recent rain had transformed the ground into a slick, uneven path, and with every step, her boots sank into the mud, emitting soft, squelching sounds that echoed in the stillness. She paused, a sudden crack of a twig slicing through the air, her heart leaping as she turned sharply toward the noise. The towering trees loomed overhead, their trunks thick and knotted, draped in a shroud of green moss that seemed to whisper secrets of ages past.

“Is someone there?” she called out, her voice trembling, a mixture of hope and dread threading through her words. The response was just silence, heavy and oppressive, wrapping around her like a cloak. The weight of the forest's mysteries pressed down on her, and she instinctively tucked a loose strand of hair behind her ear, feeling the chill of the damp air seep through her jacket.

The faint rustling of leaves broke the stillness, and a figure stepped into the muted light. He was tall and lean, his hair a wild mane that framed a face partially obscured by shadows. His clothes were tattered and earth-toned, blending seamlessly with the underbrush, giving him an almost spectral quality as though he had emerged directly from the forest floor.

“Are you lost?” he asked, his voice low and gravelly, like the sound of stones tumbling in a creek.

“Not lost,” she replied, forcing herself to stand taller, despite the fear curling in her stomach. “Just… searching.” The weight of her words lingered in the air, pressing against her chest. She shifted her weight, feeling the dampness of the forest floor seeping into her boots. “For something that belongs to me.”
