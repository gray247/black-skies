The air was thick with the scent of aged wood and something sweetly decayed, a reminder of the countless hours spent here, the laughter echoing in the corners now shadowed by silence.

Lily forced a smile, though it trembled at the edges, unsure if it could fully mask the weight of her hesitation. “Traffic was a nightmare,” she lied, the words tasting stale on her tongue. The walls, once vibrant with color, now faded to a melancholic grey, seemed to absorb the warmth from her body, leaving her feeling hollow.
