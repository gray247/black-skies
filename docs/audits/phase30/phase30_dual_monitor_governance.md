# Phase 30 Dual-Monitor Governance

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 1 - Operational Philosophy and Governance Boundaries

## Purpose

This document governs how multi-monitor exploration may be handled during Phase 30.
It does not define layouts, implementation, or approved product topology.

## Core Status

Dual-monitor behavior is exploratory only in Phase 30 Pass 1.
It is not yet governance-approved product direction.

## Primary Authoring Monitor Principles

- Writing Surface authority remains primary
- direct drafting focus stays anchored to the primary authoring context
- authoring should not become dependent on a second screen for basic coherence

## Secondary Orchestration / Support Monitor Principles

Exploratory second-screen concepts may host:

- analysis candidates
- structure and navigation candidates
- contextual status candidates
- support/recovery candidates
- advanced analytical surfaces

They may not assume:

- primary authoring authority
- automatic mutation authority
- canonical product topology

## Systems That May Migrate to Secondary Exploratory Space

As exploratory concepts only:

- contextual intelligence
- structure and navigation context
- advanced analysis
- support and recovery context
- low-priority status systems

## Systems That Stay Anchored to Writing Surface Authority

- primary drafting
- immediate narrative focus
- direct prose attention
- the clearest current authoring target

## Temporary vs Persistent Detached Windows

- temporary detached concepts may be explored
- persistent detached windows are not approved by this pass
- detachable behavior must not be used as a shortcut around authority design

## Anti-Chaos Rules

- more windows do not equal better workflow
- second-screen exploration must reduce overload, not relocate it
- no monitor becomes a dumping ground for unresolved authority decisions

## Anti-Window-Sprawl Rules

- detached concepts require explicit authority rationale
- panel multiplication is not allowed as a substitute for governance
- secondary-space exploration must still obey progressive disclosure and calmness constraints

## Authority Separation Rules

- primary authoring authority does not migrate casually
- support and diagnostics remain distinct even in secondary space
- experimental systems do not gain legitimacy merely because they are moved off the main screen

## Contextual Workflow Rules

- contextual systems may be explored in secondary space only if they remain clearly subordinate to Writing Surface authority
- secondary placement does not automatically promote a system into Command Center law
