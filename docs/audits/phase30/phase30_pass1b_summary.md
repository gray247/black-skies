## Major Structural Cognition Discoveries

- narrative understanding is increasingly governed as dynamic structure rather than static outline maintenance
- movement and manipulation are part of cognition, not just UI convenience
- passive structural signaling is preferred over intrusive analytics or grading systems
- orchestration space is best understood as structural cognition space, not overflow capacity
- Writing Surface sovereignty remains dominant even as structural exploration deepens

## Terminology Tensions

- `outline` remains directionally insufficient for the emerging system concept
- `Narrative Structure System` is useful as a directional term, but not yet fixed product law
- `Narrative Cognition` is useful as a philosophy term, but not yet a finalized subsystem label
- `topology-aware orchestration` describes useful behavior directionally, but should not be treated as an implementation commitment

## Unresolved Architecture Questions

- whether the Narrative Structure System becomes a named primary product layer or remains a guidance concept
- whether Story Units are sufficient as the structural object set
- how structural experimentation and active structure would coexist without duplicating authority
- how much relationship/topology visibility is useful before additional architectural commitments become necessary

## Likely Future Topology Exploration Areas

- movement consequence visibility
- passive dependency and fragility cues
- summonable relationship views
- cluster/isolation signaling
- structural pressure and escalation interpretation

## Likely Mockup Exploration Areas

- structural rails versus contextual overlays
- summonable depth versus persistent structural presence
- passive iconography and bounded visual signaling
- second-monitor orchestration calmness
- pinning or promotion behavior for structural tools

## Risks Of Implementation Too Early

- collapsing the system back into static outline behavior
- overcommitting to topology visuals without governance maturity
- turning structural cognition into graph spaghetti
- converting interpretive instruments into fake certainty systems
- allowing second-space orchestration to become an unbounded control room
