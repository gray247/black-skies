# Phase 30 Pass 2 Workflow Policy Summary

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 2 - Workflow Policy and Story Unit Governance

## Story Unit Governance Conclusions

- Story Units are approved as a conceptual and workflow-governed narrative object.
- Story Units are not yet approved as a canonical persisted runtime structure.
- Story Units may originate from highlighted prose, blank insertions, fragments, and notes.
- Story Units are governed as structural story-thinking objects, not as automatic replacements for scenes, drafts, or outlines.
- merge, split, rearrange, and bridge are in-bounds workflow concepts, but not approved automated intelligence actions.

## Workflow Ownership Conclusions

- Writing Surface remains primary for drafting and direct authoring mutation.
- contextual systems remain subordinate and state-triggered
- Command Center remains analysis-first and structure-first
- support and recovery remain exception-path authority
- diagnostics and dev systems remain fenced from ordinary workflow
- intelligence and mutation ownership remain explicitly separated

## Intelligence Interaction Conclusions

- advisory and descriptive intelligence remain distinct from prescriptive authority
- runtime-backed intelligence remains below qualitative validation
- rewrite/apply remains validate-first
- recommendation systems may suggest, but not silently commit or inherit authority

## Unresolved Implementation Questions

- runtime state model for Story Units
- interaction mechanics for capture, merge, split, and reorganization
- command/search integration details
- exact surface or zone placement for Story Unit operations

## Unresolved Persistence Questions

- whether Story Units are canonical persisted objects or a workflow layer over existing scene/draft structures
- what persistence authority would own Story Units if they become first-class
- how Story Units would relate durably to scenes, drafts, outline nodes, and metadata
- what undo/delete guarantees would apply once persistence exists

## Validate-First Carry-Forward Areas

- `P29-SURF-010` critique modal with rewrite/apply flow
- `P29-DEV-005` diagnostics bridge and diagnostic specs
- any intelligence-assisted Story Unit merge/split/reposition behavior

## Likely Candidate Phase 32 Trigger Areas

- if Story Units cannot be governed conceptually without defining persistence authority
- if intelligence-assisted mutation authority cannot be bounded at the workflow-policy level alone
- if qualitative validation requirements for Story Insights, Companion, or future Story Unit guidance exceed what Phase 30 can govern without a separate evaluation foundation
