# Structural Orchestration Space

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 1B - Narrative Structure System and Narrative Cognition Exploration

## Purpose

This document governs the emerging orchestration and cognition space associated with structural work.
It does not approve a final monitor layout, shell layout, or implementation design.

## Orchestration-Space Purpose

Structural orchestration space exists to support:

- narrative organization
- relationship visibility
- structural comparison
- passive comprehension
- manipulation-oriented reasoning

It is not a default dashboard or telemetry dump.

## Distinction From Writing Surface

- Writing Surface owns prose attention and direct drafting continuity
- orchestration space owns structural exploration, comparison, and manipulation support when the writer intentionally shifts focus
- orchestration space must remain subordinate to Writing Surface sovereignty

## Distinction From Dashboarding

Orchestration space is not a dashboard.
Its job is not to permanently display maximum system information.
Its job is to provide usable structural cognition and manipulation context when needed.

## Structural Exploration Behavior

- structural exploration may widen visibility when the user is comparing, rearranging, or diagnosing narrative relationships
- exploration should still remain calm and bounded
- exploration must not normalize persistent clutter

## Contextual Exploration Behavior

- orchestration space should respond to workflow state
- different structural tasks may justify different levels of visibility
- contextual depth is preferred over ambient density

## Orchestration Calmness Principles

- no infinite control room
- no constant wall of state
- no permanent requirement to monitor multiple simultaneous dashboards
- structural density must remain purposeful

## Acceptable Instrumentation Exposure

Acceptable exposure includes:

- bounded passive indicators
- relationship hints
- structural pressure cues
- dependency visibility
- movement consequence visibility

Instrumentation should explain structure, not perform status theater.

## Acceptable Topology Exposure

Topology exposure is acceptable when it clarifies:

- structural position
- pressure concentration
- dependency shape
- missing bridges
- isolation or overclustering

It is not acceptable when it exists only to signal complexity.

## Movement and Manipulation Expectations

- manipulation should feel direct and intelligible
- movement should expose structural consequence
- reorganization should remain a reasoning tool, not a hidden implementation burden

## Structural Continuity Expectations

- structural work should preserve continuity with the writer's current narrative context
- orchestration space should help the writer return to prose with greater clarity, not greater confusion
- second-space exploration must remain coupled to author understanding, not separated into an opaque control plane

## No-Overflow-Dump Governance

- orchestration space must not become the place where unresolved UI pieces, telemetry leftovers, or speculative surfaces accumulate
- every occupant of orchestration space still requires authority justification

## No-Infinite-Control-Room Governance

- more controls are not automatically more orchestration
- structural intelligence should compress thought, not multiply monitoring obligations
- orchestration space must remain legible, finite, and cognitively bounded
