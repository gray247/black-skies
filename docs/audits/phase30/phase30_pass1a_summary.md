# Phase 30 Pass 1A Summary

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 1A - Workflow Philosophy Codification

## Major Philosophical Conclusions

- Writing Surface sovereignty is now explicitly codified as the dominant workflow rule.
- Contextual intelligence is preferred over persistent intelligence clutter.
- Progressive disclosure is reaffirmed as an operational rule, not only a design preference.
- Interpretive instruments are directionally acceptable; grading systems and story scores are directionally rejected.
- Dual-monitor workflow remains a strong candidate direction, but not product law.
- Action authority and telemetry authority must remain distinct.
- Structural rails and contextual surfaces must not collapse into a persistent cockpit shell.

## Unresolved Tensions

- exact threshold between contextual and persistent structural tooling
- exact relationship between companion presence and summonable intelligence
- exact role of pinned surfaces before later workflow passes define stronger visibility rules
- exact boundary between acceptable authoring telemetry and overexposed instrumentation

## Future Risk Areas

- dual-monitor overflow-dump drift
- dashboard creep through accumulated status and instrumentation
- duplicated authority controls across rails, bars, and contextual panes
- fake certainty returning through disguised analytics language
- telemetry overpowering action hierarchy

## Candidate Follow-Up Passes

- visibility rules and progressive disclosure policy refinement
- command-style access and summonable intelligence policy
- structural rails versus contextual panels clarification
- pinned-state governance refinement if later passes require it

## Areas Requiring Later Human Review

- how far contextual intelligence may remain visible near the Writing Surface
- what bottom action authority, if any, is appropriate without creating duplication
- which structural systems deserve persistent versus summonable presence
- whether second-monitor structural rails stay contextual or become stronger future direction
