# Phase 30 Mockup Governance Rules

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 1 - Operational Philosophy and Governance Boundaries

## Purpose

This document governs how mockups, concept boards, and visual explorations may be interpreted during Phase 30.
Its purpose is to stop exploratory imagery from silently becoming architecture, roadmap law, or implementation expectation.

## What Mockups May Explore

Mockups may explore:

- information grouping
- visual hierarchy
- calmness versus density
- contextual layering
- progressive disclosure
- Writing Surface protection
- Command Center candidate tone
- dual-monitor exploratory concepts
- advanced/support/contextual separation concepts

## What Mockups May Not Imply

Mockups may not imply:

- approved workflow authority
- approved product topology
- approved panel count
- approved Story Unit authority
- approved intelligence authority
- approved mutation placement
- implementation-ready design
- runtime-backed capability

Mockups do not automatically become roadmap law.

## Concept Types

- `exploratory visual concept`: explores mood, grouping, density, or emphasis only
- `workflow concept`: explores a possible sequence or authority relationship, but is not approved policy
- `governance-approved direction`: explicitly accepted policy direction that future work may treat as guidance
- `implementation-approved design`: a later phase output that is ready to constrain build work

## Labeling Rules

Every mockup or concept artifact used in Phase 30 should be labelable as one or more of:

- `exploratory`
- `experimental`
- `future-only`
- `validate-first`
- `governance-approved`

`binding` language is not allowed for mockup artifacts in Pass 1.

## Experimental Labeling Rules

- experimental systems shown in a mockup remain experimental
- visibility inside a concept does not upgrade status
- Split Command and any similar shell concepts must retain experimental status unless a later pass explicitly changes it

## Future-Only Labeling Rules

- future-only concepts may be shown as option space
- future-only concepts must not be discussed as current workflow or approved build direction

## Validate-First Labeling Rules

- validate-first systems may be shown only if their unresolved status is preserved
- mockups must not normalize validate-first systems into confident, high-authority defaults

## Dual-Monitor Exploration Rules

- multi-monitor concepts are allowed as exploratory visual territory
- dual-monitor depiction does not mean approved product topology
- second-screen concepts cannot assume detached-window persistence, authority, or default availability

## Authority-Boundary Rules

- mockups must preserve a distinction between primary authoring authority and subordinate systems
- support, diagnostics, and experimental surfaces must not be visually merged into ordinary authoring authority without explicit justification
- intelligence surfaces must not be drawn as unquestioned authority merely because they look polished

## Anti-Cockpit Rules

- density is not a success metric
- visible simultaneous tooling must be justified by authority role, not by visual ambition
- multiple active panels do not prove a stronger workflow

## Anti-Panel-Accumulation Rules

- adding a panel in a mockup requires an authority rationale
- mockups may not assume every current or imagined surface survives
- panel count should contract when authority can be layered, deferred, or backgrounded
