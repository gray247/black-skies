# Topology and Relationship Governance

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 1B - Narrative Structure System and Narrative Cognition Exploration

## Purpose

This document governs how topology and relationship visibility may be explored without collapsing into graph theater or visual gimmicks.

## Topology Philosophy

Topology is useful when it helps writers perceive structural arrangement, dependency, distance, sequence, clustering, and pressure.
It is not useful merely because it looks dynamic.

## Relationship Visualization Boundaries

Relationship visibility may help expose:

- dependency
- adjacency
- fragility
- clustering
- separation
- isolation
- unresolved links

It must not imply stronger interpretive authority than the system can explain.

## Acceptable Visual Signaling Concepts

Directionally acceptable concepts include:

- passive iconography
- subtle proximity indicators
- bounded connective traces
- pressure or fragility hints
- cluster or isolation cues

These are exploration concepts, not mandatory product features.

## Passive Iconography Principles

- iconography should support fast comprehension without turning into symbol soup
- icons should clarify structural state, not become decorative telemetry
- passive symbols are preferable to loud diagnostic widgets

## Visual Hierarchy Rules

- narrative material must remain more important than its instrumentation
- structural signals should stay subordinate to the objects they describe
- visual density should track relevance, not technical possibility

## Anti-Graph-Spaghetti Governance

- relationship systems must not degrade into unreadable line clutter
- more visible connections do not equal better understanding
- topology exploration must privilege clarity over exhaustiveness

## Anti-Fake-Dynamic-Behavior Governance

- movement or animation should not simulate intelligence the system does not possess
- dynamic visuals must not imply live understanding, prediction, or judgment beyond evidence
- visual activity is not evidence of narrative cognition

## Structural Clarity Principles

- every visual signal should have an interpretable purpose
- relationship views should answer structural questions rather than create new ambiguity
- the system should reduce cognitive burden, not shift it into deciphering visuals

## Orchestration-Space Governance

Topology and relationship exploration belong naturally in orchestration space when they exceed the calmness budget of the Writing Surface.
That does not make orchestration space an unlimited container for all possible structure visuals.

## Narrative Movement Visibility

Movement visibility should help the writer understand what changed structurally after reordering or regrouping narrative material.
It should clarify consequences of manipulation rather than merely confirm that objects moved.

## Unresolved Visualization Questions

- how much relationship visibility is helpful before it becomes distracting
- what passive signaling vocabulary is most legible without creating icon overload
- when topology exploration should remain summonable versus pinned versus persistent
- how far structural movement visibility can go before it requires stronger architectural commitments
