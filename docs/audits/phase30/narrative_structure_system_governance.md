# Narrative Structure System Governance

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 1B - Narrative Structure System and Narrative Cognition Exploration

## Purpose

This document governs the emerging Narrative Structure System concept at the workflow and cognition level.
It does not define implementation architecture, persistence schemas, or a final product taxonomy.

## What The Narrative Structure System Conceptually Is

The Narrative Structure System is the conceptual layer that helps writers manipulate, inspect, relate, and reorganize narrative material as living structure rather than as a static outline artifact.
It is a story-shaping system oriented around movement, adjacency, relationship, continuity, escalation, and structural pressure.

It is a candidate architectural direction, not yet a finalized runtime structure.

## What It Is Not

It is not:

- a traditional static outline with richer cosmetics
- a graph gimmick or visual novelty layer
- a permanent analytics dashboard
- a grading system
- a substitute for Writing Surface sovereignty
- a license to create uncontrolled corkboard sprawl

## Relationship to Story Units

Story Units are the most likely narrative objects participating inside the Narrative Structure System.
The system governs how those units may be arranged, related, grouped, split, merged, and repositioned conceptually.
This does not yet mean Story Units are canonical persisted structure or the only possible structural object.

## Relationship to Author Cognition

The system exists to support author cognition, not to replace it.
Its role is to externalize narrative reasoning so the writer can see relationships, gaps, dependencies, and pressure patterns that are difficult to hold entirely in working memory.

## Relationship to Orchestration

The Narrative Structure System is orchestration-adjacent, but not orchestration-for-orchestration’s-sake.
It should help writers manipulate structure intentionally, not flood them with structural machinery.

## Relationship to Topology

Topology matters because narrative understanding is often about arrangement, distance, clustering, imbalance, sequence, dependency, and movement.
The system may eventually expose topology-aware views or signals, but topology concepts do not automatically become mandatory product law.

## Relationship to Timelines and Outlines

Traditional outlines and timelines remain useful reference structures, but the discovered direction exceeds them.
The current term `outline` is therefore directionally insufficient for the full system concept.
That does not mean outlines disappear.
It means the system must not be reduced back into static outline management by default.

## Movement and Manipulation Philosophy

Movement is part of narrative cognition.
Reordering, regrouping, placing-between, and relating material are not secondary conveniences; they are core reasoning actions.

Rules:

- drag/drop is a first-class workflow primitive
- manipulation should reveal structure, not merely rearrange cards
- movement should feel like structural thought, not clerical sorting

## Structural Fluidity Philosophy

Narrative structure must remain fluid long enough for discovery.
The system should let writers move between fragment, unit, scene, and structural interpretation without forcing premature rigidity.

## Passive Structural Signaling Philosophy

Passive signals are preferred to intrusive structural systems.
Useful signals may indicate fragility, imbalance, sequence pressure, orphaned dependencies, or unclosed loops without demanding constant user response.

## Anti-Static-Outline Philosophy

The system must not treat story structure as a fixed ladder that is merely being filled in.
Narrative understanding is dynamic.
The tooling should preserve room for reshaping rather than reenforcing a rigid predetermined structure too early.

## Anti-Corkboard-Chaos Philosophy

A manipulation-first system must not collapse into corkboard chaos.

Rules:

- movement requires structural clarity, not just freedom
- free placement is not an excuse for ungoverned spatial sprawl
- visual looseness must still preserve narrative legibility

## Interaction Sovereignty Principles

- Writing Surface sovereignty remains dominant
- structural systems must support, not overrun, authoring attention
- manipulation interfaces must not compete with prose focus during active drafting unless the writer intentionally shifts into structural work

## Contextual Structure Visibility Rules

- structural visibility should expand when the user is organizing, comparing, or exploring relationships
- structural visibility should contract during focused drafting
- passive signals are preferred over dense always-on structural instrumentation

## Canonical vs Alternate Structure Concerns

The system may eventually need to distinguish between:

- a currently active working structure
- alternate structural experiments
- local what-if rearrangements

This pass does not define a canonical structure model or alternate structure persistence behavior.

## Unresolved Architecture Questions

- whether the Narrative Structure System becomes a named primary product layer or remains a directional concept
- whether Story Units are sufficient as the structural object set or whether additional structural objects are needed
- how structure experimentation, active structure, and draft continuity would relate without duplicating authority
- how far topology-aware exploration can go before requiring persistence and evaluation decisions that exceed Phase 30
