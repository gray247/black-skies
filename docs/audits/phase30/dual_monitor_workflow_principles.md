# Dual Monitor Workflow Principles

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 1A - Workflow Philosophy Codification

## Purpose

This document codifies the workflow principles for dual-monitor exploration.
It governs conceptual separation only and does not approve a final product topology.

## Responsibilities Of The Primary Monitor

- maintain Writing Surface sovereignty
- preserve prose continuity
- minimize competing authority signals
- keep local drafting context visually dominant

## Responsibilities Of The Secondary Monitor

- host structural interpretation, navigation, or bounded orchestration candidates
- support contextual exploration that would otherwise crowd the Writing Surface
- hold advanced analysis without forcing it into constant authoring view

## What Should Never Permanently Occupy Monitor One

- dense telemetry walls
- permanent grading systems
- duplicate authority controls
- always-on secondary interpretation that competes with prose
- dev/test or diagnostics surfaces presented as routine authoring tools

## What Belongs Naturally On Monitor Two

- structural rails
- summonable intelligence once intentionally promoted
- advanced narrative instrumentation
- bounded support or recovery context during active exception states
- secondary navigation and organization tasks

## Monitor-Specific Cognitive Load Expectations

- monitor one should carry the lowest noise and clearest hierarchy
- monitor two may be denser, but must remain governed and legible
- increased screen space does not justify unbounded surface accumulation

## Support vs Orchestration Separation

- support and recovery context may appear on a secondary monitor when relevant
- orchestration candidates should not absorb support authority
- second-screen presence must not erase the distinction between analysis, support, and experimentation

## No-Overflow-Dump Rule

Monitor two must not become the place where unresolved ideas, unused panels, telemetry leftovers, or speculative controls are dumped.
A second screen is not a license to avoid authority decisions.

## Secondary-Monitor Contextual Exploration Principles

- secondary visibility should still be state-sensitive
- not every structural or intelligence surface deserves permanent display
- pinning or promotion should remain intentional rather than ambient

## Writing Continuity Preservation

- switching attention away from the Writing Surface should be optional, not mandatory
- second-screen exploration must reduce clutter on monitor one, not merely relocate it
- the writer should always be able to return to an uncluttered primary drafting state
