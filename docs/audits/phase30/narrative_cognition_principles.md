# Narrative Cognition Principles

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 1B - Narrative Structure System and Narrative Cognition Exploration

## Purpose

This document defines how the system should support narrative cognition without turning interpretation into judgment or clutter.

## Cognition-Support Philosophy

The product should help the writer think about story structure more clearly, not merely store structural data.
Narrative cognition support means making hidden structural relationships easier to perceive, compare, and manipulate.

## Externalized Narrative Reasoning

The system should let authors move part of their structural reasoning into the environment:

- what depends on what
- what feels unsupported
- what clusters together
- what feels out of place
- where pressure escalates or dissipates

This is externalized reasoning, not automated judgment.

## Structural Understanding Support

Structural understanding support should help the writer see:

- sequence
- dependency
- continuity
- placement tension
- unresolved gaps
- competing pressure patterns

## Gap Visibility Philosophy

Gap visibility should expose missing connective material, unsupported transitions, absent setup, or broken continuity.
It should not present gaps as final proof of bad writing.

## Fragility Discovery Philosophy

Fragility discovery should reveal where the narrative may break under scrutiny:

- unsupported turns
- weak transitions
- isolated units
- stalled escalation
- disconnected payoffs

Fragility is a structural discovery concept, not a quality score.

## Pacing and Tension Topology Concepts

Pacing and tension are understood as patterns across structure, not just as local sentence qualities.
The system may help expose where pacing compresses, flattens, clusters, or evaporates across units or sequences.

## Dependency Visibility Concepts

Dependency visibility should help the writer notice:

- which units rely on earlier setup
- which ideas remain unpaid off
- which sequences require bridging material
- which structural moves destabilize other parts

## Relationship Awareness Concepts

Relationship awareness is broader than character graphs.
It includes links among:

- units
- reveals
- motifs
- threads
- setup/payoff sequences
- cause/effect structure

## Narrative Pressure Concepts

Narrative pressure refers to how story weight accumulates, transfers, or dissipates across structure.
Useful systems may expose where too much pressure sits in one region or where escalation collapses prematurely.

## Interruption Minimization Principles

- cognitive aids should avoid interrupting drafting unless the user is already in a structural task
- passive visibility is preferable to constant prompting
- the system should reveal truth without demanding reaction every time it sees a pattern

## Passive Comprehension Enhancement

The best narrative cognition aids improve comprehension quietly.
They help the writer notice structure sooner, with less friction, and with fewer explicit mode shifts.

## Reveal Truth, Do Not Dictate

The governing principle is:

reveal truth, do not dictate

That means:

- expose patterns
- surface tensions
- clarify relationships
- preserve author agency
- avoid verdicts
- avoid scoring
- avoid performative certainty
