# Phase 30 Intelligence Interaction Policy

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-05-22
Phase: 30 - GUI / Workflow Realignment Spec
Pass: 2 - Workflow Policy and Story Unit Governance

## Purpose

This policy defines how intelligence systems may interact with author workflow at the governance level.
It does not define implementation, routing internals, or final UI placement.

## Runtime Truth Rule

Runtime-backed does not equal qualitatively validated.
Phase 30 inherits that rule without weakening it.

## Advisory vs Prescriptive Intelligence

- descriptive intelligence may summarize or surface observable patterns
- advisory intelligence may suggest questions, directions, or concerns
- prescriptive intelligence implies stronger authority and therefore requires stronger validation and tighter governance

Pass 2 does not approve any current system as fully trusted prescriptive authority.

## Contextual Intelligence Behavior

- contextual intelligence may assist the current task without displacing Writing Surface primacy
- contextual intelligence should enter when relevant and recede when not
- contextual intelligence must not feel like a permanently active supervisor

## Interruption Thresholds

- interruptions should be rare and justified
- low-confidence, low-frequency, or weakly proven intelligence should not interrupt author flow
- support and recovery interruptions are governed separately from intelligence interruptions

## Visibility Thresholds

- descriptive metrics may survive with lower authority than advisory systems
- advisory surfaces must justify visibility by usefulness, not by novelty
- prescriptive or mutation-adjacent intelligence must meet the highest scrutiny

## Rewrite / Apply Governance

- rewrite/apply remains validate-first
- rewrite/apply is not normalized here into ordinary authoring flow
- any future use of rewrite/apply must preserve explicit review and trust framing
- no invisible or casual escalation from advice into mutation is allowed

## Confidence Communication Expectations

- uncertainty must remain legible
- low-confidence or partial analysis must not present as narrative truth
- status and provenance are not substitutes for qualitative validation

## Qualitative Validation Boundaries

- Story Insights, Companion, and any future Story Unit guidance may remain visible only within the limits defined by Phase 29 and later policy
- any promotion into prescriptive authority may require later qualitative validation and possibly Candidate Phase 32 consideration

## Recommendation Behavior

- recommendations may suggest, compare, or question
- recommendations may not silently commit structural or textual changes
- recommendations should be framed as optional guidance rather than implicit next steps

## Background Automation Candidates

- routing metadata, low-visibility helpers, or similar support systems may stay in the background
- background automation should reduce overload, not create hidden authority confusion
- background status may surface when needed for trust or clarity

## Workflow Calmness Protections

- intelligence must not turn the product into a constant commentary layer
- authoring flow remains protected from dense simultaneous intelligence pressure
- multiple intelligence surfaces should not compete for the same authority moment

## Anti-Fake-Smart Rules

- polished presentation does not earn authority
- branding must not imply stronger capability than proof supports
- speculative or partial analysis must not be styled or framed as final judgment

## Mutation Authority Escalation Risks

- intelligence-assisted mutation is a separate risk class from descriptive analytics
- escalation from advice into action requires explicit user review
- intelligence systems must not inherit mutation authority simply because they are nearby, convenient, or repeated in mockups
