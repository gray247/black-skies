## Purpose

This document defines the formal conditions required to close Phase 29 and allow Phase 30 to begin.

- Pass 1 inventory artifacts

- Pass 6 closure readiness, carry-forward, human review, and dependency artifacts

- explicit no-fantasy-promotion enforcement

## Required Carry-Forward Handling

- unresolved issues must be preserved in `phase29_carry_forward_register.md`
- carry-forward items must declare future owner phase and blocking/nonblocking status
- unresolved issues must not be silently normalized into approved workflow policy

## Required Human Review Checkpoints

Before final closure is accepted, operator review must explicitly examine:

- Workspace Header authority decomposition
- rewrite/apply trust boundary
- support-versus-diagnostics separation
- Story Insights and Companion visibility authority
- Split Command future legitimacy
- export workflow authority
- command-style access visibility
- snapshots/recovery visibility
- contextual-versus-background intelligence
- Writing Surface authority boundaries

## Unresolved but Acceptable Conditions

- command-style access remains unresolved as long as it is not promoted to approved workflow authority
- export workflow authority remains unresolved as long as it stays capped by governance classification
- diagnostics bridge promotion remains unresolved as long as dev/test and support boundaries are preserved explicitly

## Unacceptable Unresolved Conditions

- missing or unstable classification IDs
- missing governance disposition for major authority surfaces
- missing carry-forward ownership for high-risk unresolved items
- treating validate-first or deferred systems as approved workflow authority
- beginning Phase 30 from implied or speculative assumptions rather than recorded Phase 29 evidence

## Exact Criteria Allowing Phase 30 to Begin

Phase 30 may begin when:

- all required Phase 29 artifacts exist
- no Phase 29 stop condition remains unresolved
- carry-forward ownership is explicit
- operator review checkpoints are acknowledged as mandatory inputs
- Phase 30 agrees to use Phase 29 as governance evidence rather than final product policy
