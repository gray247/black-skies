## Survivability Classification Model

These are reconstruction-planning classifications only. They are not implementation approval, not product workflow states, and not roadmap law.

- `Survives Intact`: the core work still fits reconstruction-era governance with little conceptual change.
- `Survives With Redesign`: the core need survives, but its current expression is likely to be reshaped later.
- `Transitional`: useful as current continuity evidence, but exposed to later replacement.
- `Maintenance Only`: safe to preserve or repair narrowly without directional expansion.
- `Governance Blocked`: future extension depends on unresolved authority boundaries.
- `Workflow Blocked`: future extension depends on unresolved workflow-state or workflow-shape decisions.
- `Reconstruction Dependent`: potentially valuable, but cannot be trusted as a forward-planning basis until broader reconstruction decisions land.
- `Superseded Candidate`: a historically meaningful direction that is now under meaningful replacement pressure.
- `Unsafe To Continue`: should not be extended now because it risks contaminating future workflow/governance.
- `Historical Only`: evidence value remains, but the work should not guide active implementation direction directly.
