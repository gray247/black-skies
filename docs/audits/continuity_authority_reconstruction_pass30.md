## Continuity Authority Model

This model is provisional only. It is not persistence authorization, not topology authorization, not structural canon, and not workflow-state canon.

Continuity authority means the ability to claim that narrative, structural, selected, grouped, generated, rewritten, recovered, or reopened material still belongs to the same meaningful continuity across change.

Core distinctions:
- continuity pressure is not persistence authorization
- grouped continuity is not stable identity
- continuity inheritance is not source-of-truth canon
- narrative continuity is not structural continuity
- recomputed continuity is not durable continuity identity
- continuity visibility is not permission to mutate

Provisional continuity families:
- `Narrative Continuity`: meaning, voice, sequence, character, causality, and draft flow across writing changes.
- `Structural Continuity`: scene-like, outline-like, grouped, or hierarchical continuity across organization changes.
- `Execution Continuity`: continuity carried through generation, rewrite/apply, restore, reopen, or recovery.
- `Selection Continuity`: continuity implied when selected material transfers between states.
- `Batch Continuity`: continuity implied across multiple grouped items.
- `Source Continuity`: continuity implied by loaded, recovered, stale, or reopened state. This remains deferred to source-of-truth reconstruction.

## Executive Findings

- Continuity authority is a separate governance family from persistence and source of truth.
- Generation, rewrite/apply, restore, reopen, advisory review, and structural grouping all pressure continuity assumptions before structural canon exists.
- Narrative continuity can remain useful as a writing concern without authorizing Story Unit persistence.
- Structural continuity is higher risk because repeated scene-like, outline-like, or grouped continuity can imply durable object identity.
- Recomputed groupings must not be treated as durable continuity identity.
- Recovery continuity is especially sensitive because restore/reopen can change the source of truth while appearing to preserve continuity.
- Structural mutation governance should be reconstructed next because move, split, merge, reorder, and batch structural operations depend on continuity authority.

## Narrative Continuity Pressure Findings

### Drafting Continuity

- Drafting continuity is the ordinary sense that writing remains coherent across local edits.
- It should not imply persistent structural units.
- Focused Drafting can preserve narrative continuity without stabilizing workflow-state canon.
- Drafting continuity becomes risky when selection, generation, or structural labels imply durable units beyond the text itself.

### Rewrite Continuity

- Rewrite continuity asks whether transformed material is still the same passage, scene, intention, or narrative moment.
- Rewrite / Apply can preserve, alter, or break continuity depending on scope.
- A candidate rewrite is not continuity approval.
- Apply authority needs continuity boundary questions before mutation, especially when selected source and affected range differ.

### Generation Continuity

- Generation continuity asks whether new material continues, inserts into, replaces, or broadens existing narrative.
- Generation scope changes continuity scale and blast radius.
- Runtime readiness does not certify continuity quality.
- Generated material should not inherit existing continuity authority unless the transition is explicitly governed.

### Advisory Continuity

- Advisory continuity appears when critique or analysis claims that a change would preserve, improve, or repair narrative flow.
- Advisory continuity is not mutation authority.
- Advisory confidence can pressure rewrite/apply even when it only describes continuity.
- Advanced Analysis inherits this risk when it frames structural or narrative continuity as judgment.

### Review Continuity

- Review continuity can compare current draft, candidate rewrite, generated result, or inspection evidence.
- Review can describe continuity gaps without authorizing repair.
- Review language should not convert continuity findings into apply permission.
- Verification remains evidence, not continuity approval.

### Recovery Continuity

- Recovery continuity asks whether restored, reopened, copied, or repaired state remains meaningfully connected to current work.
- Restore/reopen can preserve apparent continuity while changing source-of-truth authority.
- Recovery continuity must remain exceptional and cannot authorize recovery mutation by itself.
- Recovery continuity depends on later stale-state/source-of-truth reconstruction.

## Structural Continuity Pressure Findings

### Grouped Structure Continuity

- Grouped structure continuity appears when grouped scenes, beats, passages, or clusters seem to remain the same across operations.
- Grouped continuity can imply Story Unit identity without persistence authorization.
- Grouped continuity should remain pressure only until object identity and source-of-truth rules stabilize.

### Hierarchy Continuity

- Hierarchy continuity appears when parent/child, order, level, or sequence seems stable across revisions.
- Hierarchy can imply topology or canonical organization.
- Hierarchy continuity remains pressure only until topology containment and structural canon are reconstructed.

### Outline Continuity

- Outline continuity appears when outline-like items seem to survive generation, rewrite/apply, recovery, or regrouping.
- Outline items may be recomputed views rather than durable units.
- Recomputed outline continuity must not be treated as persistent identity.

### Scene-Like Continuity

- Scene-like continuity feels natural, but naturalness is not persistence authorization.
- Scene-like identity and outline/object identity remain grouped for now.
- Scene-like continuity becomes risky when it is selectable, searchable, commandable, or batch-grouped.

### Recomputed Grouping Continuity

- Recomputed groupings may look stable because labels or positions repeat.
- Recomputed continuity is not durable continuity identity.
- Recomputed structures require later source-of-truth analysis before they can support structural search or mutation.

### Continuity Across Restore/Reopen/Generation

- Restore can preserve previous continuity while disrupting current continuity.
- Reopen can appear to continue a project while changing session or source authority.
- Generation can create continuity claims without proving continuity.
- Structural continuity across these operations remains unresolved until stale-state/source-of-truth work happens.

## Continuity Inheritance Findings

- Continuity can be inherited from active draft, selected context, advisory framing, generated output, rewrite candidate, verification evidence, recovery source, or structural grouping.
- Inheritance must not be automatic.
- Selection inheritance can carry continuity assumptions from one state to another.
- Command/search inheritance must not expose continuity-bearing structural routes before structural governance.
- Recovery inheritance must distinguish prior state continuity from current source-of-truth authority.
- Batch continuity must distinguish per-item continuity from set-level continuity.

## Continuity Mutation Pressure Findings

- Mutation pressure appears when continuity claims motivate generation, rewrite/apply, restore, reopen, repair, move, split, merge, reorder, or batch transformation.
- Continuity judgment is not mutation permission.
- Structural mutation pressure should split from general Story Unit pressure next.
- Mutation-Boundary Authority must eventually ask what continuity is preserved, broken, created, replaced, or abandoned.
- Structural mutation remains blocked until continuity, source-of-truth, topology, and persistence boundaries stabilize.

## Continuity Visibility / Legitimacy Drift Findings

- Visible continuity labels, summaries, scores, groupings, or sequence markers can imply authority.
- Repeated continuity displays can train users to trust recomputed groupings as durable identity.
- Continuity language can imply quality certification when it is only advisory.
- Continuity shown near apply, generate, restore, or reopen controls can become mutation pressure.
- Structural continuity visibility can normalize Story Unit-like identity without authorization.

## Continuity Versus Persistence Findings

- Continuity means material appears meaningfully connected across change.
- Persistence means identity is durably stored, addressable, and authoritative.
- Continuity pressure can exist without persistence.
- Persistence-looking surfaces can turn continuity pressure into implied Story Unit authority.
- Continuity reconstruction should contain persistence pressure, not authorize it.
- Story Unit persistence remains unauthorized.

## Continuity Versus Source-Of-Truth Findings

- Continuity is not source-of-truth canon.
- A restored or reopened state can be continuous with prior work while not being the current authority.
- A generated or rewritten result can be continuous in meaning while not being applied state.
- A recomputed structural group can be continuous in analysis while not being durable data.
- Stale-state/source-of-truth reconstruction is required before continuity can govern reopened, recovered, stale, or rehydrated state.
