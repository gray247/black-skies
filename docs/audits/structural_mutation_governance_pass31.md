## Structural Mutation Governance Model

This model is provisional only. It is not persistence authorization, not topology authorization, not structural canon, and not workflow-state canon.

Structural mutation pressure means a surface, grouping, hierarchy, outline, selection, search result, generation, rewrite/apply flow, restore/reopen path, or organizational action begins to imply authority to change narrative structure rather than only text.

Core distinctions:
- structure mutation is not persistence authorization
- recomputed structure is not durable identity
- grouping mutation is not canonical hierarchy mutation
- continuity mutation is not source-of-truth authority
- organizational mutation is not automatically persistence mutation
- structural mutation pressure is not implementation necessity

Provisional structural mutation families:
- `Hierarchy Mutation`: changing parent/child, level, order, dependency, or sequence.
- `Grouping Mutation`: grouping, ungrouping, clustering, splitting, or merging narrative material.
- `Outline Mutation`: changing outline-like rows, sections, beats, or nodes.
- `Scene-Like Mutation`: moving, splitting, merging, rewriting, or regenerating scene-like material.
- `Organizational Mutation`: changing view, arrangement, ordering, categorization, or workflow organization.
- `Recomputed Structure Mutation`: changing source material in a way that changes recomputed structural views.
- `Recovery Structural Mutation`: restore/reopen/recovery changing structural interpretation or continuity.
- `Generation/Rewrite Structural Mutation`: generated or rewritten content changing implied structure.

## Executive Findings

- Structural mutation remains pressure only; it cannot stabilize without Story Unit persistence, source-of-truth, topology containment, and continuity authority work.
- Move, split, merge, reorder, group, ungroup, promote, demote, and structural batch operations all imply object identity that is not authorized.
- Organizational mutation can look harmless while creating persistence and topology pressure through hierarchy or ordering changes.
- Recomputed structural views make mutation ambiguous because changing text can appear to change structure without durable structural identity.
- Restore/reopen and generation/rewrite can mutate structural interpretation even when no explicit structural tool exists.
- Structural mutation visibility creates legitimacy drift by making structural units feel editable, persistent, and canonical.
- Stale-state/source-of-truth reconstruction should follow because structural mutation cannot be governed until authority over current, stale, recovered, and recomputed state is separated.

## Structural Mutation Pressure Findings

### Hierarchy Mutation

- Hierarchy mutation pressure appears when parent/child, order, level, dependency, or sequence can be changed.
- Hierarchy mutation can imply topology architecture even when framed as organization.
- A hierarchy change may be view-only, advisory, recomputed, or persistent; this distinction is unresolved.
- Hierarchy mutation remains blocked until topology containment and source-of-truth rules stabilize.

### Grouping Mutation

- Grouping mutation pressure appears around group, ungroup, cluster, split, merge, include, and exclude semantics.
- Grouping mutation can imply stable object boundaries.
- Grouping mutation is not canonical hierarchy mutation by default.
- Grouping may be advisory or contextual, but cannot become durable Story Unit identity without authorization.

### Outline Mutation

- Outline mutation pressure appears when outline-like items can be renamed, reordered, promoted, demoted, split, merged, or regenerated.
- Outline items may be recomputed views rather than durable units.
- Mutating an outline-like item may mutate text, structure, or only a view; the source of authority is unresolved.
- Outline mutation remains pressure only.

### Scene-Like Mutation

- Scene-like mutation feels natural because scenes are writer-familiar units.
- Naturalness does not authorize Story Unit identity or persistence.
- Scene-like move, split, merge, regenerate, or batch rewrite actions require structural mutation governance.
- Scene-like continuity and outline/object identity remain grouped for now.

### Organizational Mutation

- Organizational mutation may change sorting, grouping, labels, hierarchy, view state, or perceived workflow path.
- Some organization changes may be presentation-only while others imply durable structure.
- Organizational mutation can become persistence mutation if the organization is saved, searched, recovered, or commanded.
- Organizational mutation must be distinguished from structural persistence before canon work.

### Recomputed Structure Mutation

- Recomputed structure mutation occurs when text mutation changes derived structure.
- Recomputed changes can appear stable because the structure reappears with similar labels or positions.
- Recomputed structure is not durable identity.
- Users may still experience recomputed changes as structural mutation, so visibility and vocabulary must stay cautious.

### Restore/Reopen Structural Mutation

- Restore/reopen can change structural interpretation by reverting, forking, rehydrating, or loading different source material.
- Reopen is not harmless navigation when it changes structural continuity or source authority.
- Restore can preserve prior structure while disrupting current structure.
- Restore/reopen structural mutation remains deferred pending source-of-truth and restore/reopen split work.

### Generation/Rewrite Structural Mutation

- Generation and rewrite/apply can mutate implied structure by adding, removing, splitting, merging, or reordering narrative material.
- A text mutation can become structural mutation by effect even if not framed as structure.
- Generation/rewrite structural mutation must inherit Mutation-Boundary Authority and continuity boundary questions.
- Advisory structural suggestions are not structural mutation authority.

## Structural Mutation Authority Findings

- Structural mutation authority is not currently authorized.
- Any future authority would need target identity, source identity, destination identity, affected range, continuity impact, blast radius, persistence behavior, reversibility, and source-of-truth ownership.
- Structural mutation authority cannot be inferred from visible hierarchy, grouping, search results, selection, advisory recommendations, or generated/rewrite candidates.
- Structural mutation authority must remain separate from ordinary text mutation until source-of-truth and persistence questions are resolved.
