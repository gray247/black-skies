## 4. Core Doctrine Preserved

- `Writing Surface` and `Command Center` are separate surfaces with
  distinct workspaces.
- `Narrative Insertion / Narrative Assertion` owns accepted manuscript
  content and authoritative manuscript order.
- Scenes and chapters are projections or organizational containers, not
  manuscript foundations.
- Direct writing remains sovereign and non-gated.
- `Author Intent / Story Setup` singularly owns accepted project-level
  truth.
- `Workflow Spine / Author Journey` owns journey and workflow state
  only.
- `Character Cards` own accepted structured character truth.
- `Lore Cards` own accepted structured world truth.
- Support systems do not gain truth authority by displaying or
  analyzing information.
- AI and analyzer inference remain advisory until explicitly accepted
  through an owner-governed path.
- `Feedback Notes` own durable editorial concerns.
- `Signals` own durable attention and triage state.
- Surfaces display and request actions; durable owners mutate state.
- Prototype arrangements change labeled projections only unless
  explicitly applied.
- Protected, masked, unrevealed, private, and AI-excluded information
  must not leak through support systems.
