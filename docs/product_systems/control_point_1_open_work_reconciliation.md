## 2. Reconciliation Rule

The source repositories contain hundreds of historical `next`, `open`, and
`deferred` statements. They are evidence, not an active queue. A source item is
current only when this review gives it:

- a stable present identity,
- one present owner or resolution point,
- a trigger or exit condition,
- a blocking or non-blocking posture,
- and a next required review.

Dossier questions remain in their owning dossiers unless an authorized
workflow needs them. Historical findings already closed by the V1 exact-
candidate receipt are not reopened merely because an older paragraph says
`open`.
