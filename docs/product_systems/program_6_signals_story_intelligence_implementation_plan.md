## 4. Frozen Product Decisions

### 4.1 Postures

Signal posture controls whether and how a signal family may be evaluated or
surfaced:

- `Off`: do not watch or surface the signal family;
- `Ask Only`: evaluate only when the author explicitly requests it;
- `Quiet`: watch without interrupting and expose only a small indicator or
  summonable cue; and
- `Alert`: interrupt only when the configured, evidence-backed condition is
  met.

The default signal posture is `Ask Only`. A task must not silently promote a
project or signal family to `Quiet` or `Alert`.

Project posture describes the project's working maturity and the amount of
support the author wants:

- `Explore`: tolerate gaps and incomplete structure;
- `Develop`: surface repeated or increasingly consequential concerns quietly;
- `Finish`: increase review coverage and unresolved-issue visibility.

The default project posture is `Develop`. Project posture changes are
settings changes, not findings and not story truth.

### 4.2 Emotion and reader-effect lanes

Emotion labels are flexible author-facing labels, not a closed universal
emotion ontology. A finding may use a label such as `guarded`, `tender`,
`angry`, `relieved`, `uncertain`, or an author-defined label. Labels must not
pretend that the system has discovered a canonical inner state.

Intensity uses five qualitative bands:

1. `very-low`;
2. `low`;
3. `medium`;
4. `high`; and
5. `very-high`.

Intensity is qualitative and must not be rendered or stored as a percentage.
An absent or disputed intensity remains `unknown` rather than being forced
into a band.

These lanes remain distinct in storage, transport, rendering, and review:

- `planned`: an author-planned target from an existing planning owner;
- `observed`: a reference to manuscript or accepted-source evidence;
- `inferred`: a temporary advisory interpretation produced by deterministic
  or optional local analysis; and
- `reader-effect-optional`: an optional intended or detected reader-effect
  lane that is collapsed or absent unless the author uses it.

Planned material is not observed material. Observed material is not inferred
material. Inferred material is not accepted intent. Reader effect is not
character emotion.

### 4.3 Timeline V1

Timeline V1 includes three named support modules in one bounded lens:

- chronology: story-world chronology, manuscript order, planning order,
  reveal order, durations, simultaneity, and uncertainty;
- pacing: planned-versus-observed tempo, density, compression, expansion,
  delay, repetition, and bounded reader-experience interpretation; and
- pressure: separate urgency, consequence, constraint, and conflict
  dimensions.

Pressure must not collapse into one universal tension score. Timeline remains
support-only and does not own manuscript order, accepted chronology,
Character or Lore truth, project intent, Outline planning, Story Unit purpose,
Continuity truth, or emotional truth.

### 4.4 Surface posture

- Writing Surface remains quiet and non-gating.
- Detailed findings, evidence, comparisons, history, and safe actions belong
  in the existing Story Knowledge Command Center workspace.
- Focus hides intelligence presentation without changing signal posture,
  project posture, policies, durable signals, or any other setting.
- Direct writing remains available when intelligence presentation is hidden,
  unavailable, degraded, or disabled.
- A signal is an attention object, not accepted story truth.
- No signal, finding, note, memory, model result, or projection may silently
  mutate prose, outline, fact, canon, truth, or settings.

## 5. Ownership And Permission Doctrine

Program 6 consumes existing owners; it does not create a shadow canon.

| Material | Owner | Program 6 treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted manuscript text and order | Narrative Insertion / Narrative Assertion | Read by permitted analysis; never replaced by a finding or signal |
| Accepted assertions, lore, character facts, and project intent | Their existing truth owners | Referenced through source links; never copied into graph or signal truth |
| Outline and Story Unit planning | Outline / Story Unit | Used as planned context; planned targets remain distinct |
| Durable signal state | Signal Architecture | Created or changed only through its owner and explicit author action |
| Provenance and visibility metadata | Authorship Provenance AI Visibility | Referenced and recorded through the provenance owner |
| Settings and project preferences | Settings / Preferences / Workspace Layout | Persisted only by the settings owner |
| Candidate findings and temporary analysis | Requesting intelligence system | Temporary by default; no automatic durable conversion |
| Story Knowledge Command Center presentation | Command Center | Non-owning projection and action host |

The Writing Surface and Command Center may display findings, signals,
currentness, and provenance. They may request owner actions but may not
become owners merely by rendering or routing the result.

## 6. Shared Contract Families

The exact TypeScript names may vary only if responsibility and semantics stay
equivalent. Contract names below are the planning authority for P6-A and
later batches.

### 6.1 `SignalPostureV1`

Represents the selected delivery posture for a signal family or project.

```text
SignalPostureV1 = off | ask-only | quiet | alert
```

The value is a preference/policy input. It is not a signal lifecycle state,
impact level, or proof that a finding exists. Invalid values fail closed to a
truthful unavailable/default-safe result; they must not escalate to `alert`.

### 6.2 `ProjectPostureV1`

```text
ProjectPostureV1 = explore | develop | finish
```

The value affects review sensitivity and presentation policy only. It must
not change source evidence, accepted truth, or the lifecycle of an existing
signal without explicit owner action.

### 6.3 `StoryIntelligenceAnalysisPolicyV1`

The policy is project-local and explicit. It must carry enough information to
prove the analysis boundary without carrying prose. The planned minimum is:

```text
StoryIntelligenceAnalysisPolicyV1
  schemaVersion
  signalPosture
  projectPosture
  deterministicEnabled
  optionalInferenceEnabled
  readerEffectLaneEnabled
  allowedSourceClasses
  excludedSourceClasses
  selectedScopePolicy
  retentionPolicy
  updatedAt
```

Semantics:

- `deterministicEnabled` can be true while `optionalInferenceEnabled` is
  false;
- `optionalInferenceEnabled` is an explicit local-only policy flag, not a
  provider permission or a blanket AI approval;
- `readerEffectLaneEnabled` defaults false and does not merge reader effect
  with emotion;
- `allowedSourceClasses` and `excludedSourceClasses` are deny-by-default and
  must be validated against the protected-content policy;
- a policy cannot authorize outbound work, paid work, background work, or
  durable conversion; and
- the policy is not itself an instruction to run analysis.

### 6.4 `StoryPositionRefV1`

Every source-linked item must use the existing manuscript and structural
anchor/fingerprint discipline rather than inventing a second identity model.

```text
StoryPositionRefV1
  projectId
  sourceKind
  sourceId
  anchorId?
  revision
  startOffset?
  endOffset?
  selectionFingerprint?
  unitId?
  orderBasis?
```

Offsets are navigation evidence, not authority. A reference is stale when
its revision/fingerprint no longer matches the current source. A source that
cannot be opened is unavailable, not silently reconstructed. References may
point to manuscript, assertion, Outline, Story Unit, Character Card, Lore
Card, or author-intent owners, but they do not transfer ownership.

### 6.5 `EvidenceClassV1`

```text
EvidenceClassV1 = planned | observed | inferred | reader-effect-optional
```

The class must be carried through finding, signal, history, IPC, preload, and
Command Center projections. A consumer may filter a class, but may not merge
classes while presenting a single authoritative statement.

### 6.6 `ConfidenceBandV1`

```text
ConfidenceBandV1 = unknown | low | medium | high
```

Confidence is qualitative, not a percentage, score, probability, or truth
grade. `high` means the bounded evidence and method are strong for the stated
finding; it does not mean the interpretation is canon or certainly correct.

### 6.7 `SignalImpactV1`

```text
SignalImpactV1 = informational | attention | urgent | blocking
```

Impact controls attention posture, not source severity or truth. `blocking`
must identify the blocked support action, owning system, required action, and
available fallback. A blocking signal must not block direct writing unless a
separate owner-governed safety rule explicitly says so.

### 6.8 `SignalLifecycleV1`

```text
SignalLifecycleV1 = candidate | reviewed | accepted | dismissed | suppressed
                  | expired | converted | resolved | superseded
```

`stale` is not a lifecycle value. It is a `CurrentnessV1` condition that may
be attached to a finding or durable signal. Candidates are temporary. A
durable signal requires explicit confirmation through the Signal Architecture
owner path.

Legal transitions are:

| From | Legal next state | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `candidate` | `reviewed`, `dismissed`, `expired` | Review or candidate-expiry logic |
| `reviewed` | `accepted`, `dismissed`, `suppressed`, `expired`, `converted`, `resolved` | Explicit author/owner action |
| `accepted` | `suppressed`, `converted`, `resolved`, `expired`, `superseded` | Explicit owner action or newer evidence |
| `suppressed` | `reviewed`, `accepted`, `resolved`, `expired` | Explicit unsuppress/review or owner action |
| `dismissed` | none | A later related observation creates a fresh candidate |
| `converted` | none | Destination owner controls later state |
| `resolved` | none | A later related observation creates a fresh candidate |
| `expired` | none | A later related observation creates a fresh candidate |
| `superseded` | none | The newer signal owns current attention |

No transition silently reopens `dismissed`, `resolved`, `expired`, or
`superseded`. A possible recurrence is a new linked candidate. Lifecycle
changes never mutate source prose, facts, canon, outline, notes, memory, or
settings.

### 6.9 `CurrentnessV1`

```text
CurrentnessV1 = current | stale | unavailable | trimmed
```

Currentness is a condition on evidence or a support record. `stale` means the
record may no longer match current source or owner state and needs
revalidation. `unavailable` means the source cannot currently be opened.
`trimmed` means the historical detail is no longer retained. None of these
conditions makes a record current, accepted, or more authoritative.

## 7. Persistence Boundary

The durable file is project-local `story-intelligence.json`. It is not a
global database, hidden application cache, generic analytics log, or model
memory store.

### 7.1 Persist

Persist only:

- schema version and project identity;
- revision for optimistic concurrency;
- explicit signal posture, project posture, and analysis policy;
- unit-level policy overrides that remain within project scope;
- author-entered records and explicit dispositions;
- durable signals after explicit confirmation;
- bounded provenance references and display-safe summaries; and
- at most 200 metadata-only history events.

### 7.2 Do not persist

Do not persist:

- candidate findings;
- raw model output;
- raw model packages or prompt payloads;
- excerpts or reconstructed excerpts;
- excluded prose or raw protected content;
- reconstructed excluded summaries;
- transient graph layouts or session-only filters unless a later scoped
  presentation preference explicitly permits them;
- credentials, provider diagnostics, or outbound payloads; or
- any inferred finding as accepted truth.

### 7.3 Write protocol

Reads and writes are project-bound. A write must include the observed
`projectId`, generation, and document revision. A revision mismatch returns a
truthful conflict and leaves the file unchanged. The writer must serialize a
validated document, write a temporary sibling file, flush/close it under the
repository's existing local-save discipline, and atomically replace the
project-local target. A failed replacement must not claim success or leave a
partially written current document.

History is trimmed oldest-first after a successful accepted write, while
preserving the maximum of 200 events and retaining no raw content. A trim is
honest metadata, not an invented currentness state.
