## Implementation-Neutral Boundary

This file records constitution, batch structure, evidence standards, and deferrals.
It does not choose runtime code, tests, schemas, APIs, classes, libraries, deployment mechanics, packaging formats, or release automation.
It does not authorize implementation.

## Evidence-Driven Audit Posture

Stage 10 is evidence-driven, not intention-driven.

- Direct evidence is what the repository and approved proof records actually show.
- Planned behavior is doctrine or intent; it is not operational proof.
- Passing tests alone do not prove product correctness.
- Operational evidence must identify what was actually observed, on what revision or environment, under what scope, with what limitations and freshness.
- Historical evidence is useful only when it is clearly labeled historical.
- A claim must not exceed what was directly observed.

## Program Constitution

- The author is final authority.
- Product doctrine and ownership do not change in Stage 10.
- Analysis may be automatic; truth mutation is manual.
- Narrative Assertion remains the foundational manuscript-truth object.
- Writing Surface remains sovereign and direct writing remains primary.
- Command Center remains non-sovereign and not a universal inbox.
- Companion remains optional, contextual, and non-owning.
- Models perform tasks; systems own workflows.
- Route approval, package approval, transmission approval, execution, result receipt, and acceptance remain distinct.
- Save, snapshot, history, backup, archive, export, import, inspection, restore, and verification remain distinct.
- No connector is admitted.
- No silent fallback, substitution, transmission, retry, or truth mutation is allowed.
- Restored-copy identity remains deferred to Stage 12.
- Stage 10 audits readiness but does not authorize implementation.

## Evidence Standards

Stage 10 evidence must be concrete and bounded.

- Direct evidence should cite the source path, revision or record, scope, observed result, limitations, freshness, and protection constraints.
- Witness evidence is valid only when it is clearly labeled as bounded witness evidence.
- Historical evidence remains historical evidence.
- Planned behavior is not evidence.
- A proof record proves the boundary it actually covers; it does not prove implementation details it explicitly excludes.
- Diagnostics, tests, and fixtures are evidence only for the behavior they directly observe.

## Doctrine-Resolved Non-Decisions

- Save is not snapshot.
- Snapshot is not backup.
- Backup is not archive.
- Archive is not export.
- Inspection is not restore.
- Copying is not parsing.
- Parsing is not verification.
- Verification is not complete recovery.
- Restore-as-copy is not restore-as-current.
- Partial recovery is not success.
- Import is not acceptance.
- Degraded service is not project-load failure.
- No connector is admitted.
- No silent fallback, substitution, transmission, retry, or truth mutation is allowed.
- Restored-copy identity remains deferred to Stage 12.

## Genuine Author Decisions

- The exact threshold between pending, degraded, at-risk, blocked, and failed current-save posture.
- The exact safe-mode or startup-bypass posture when bad settings or broken subsystems cause crash loops.
- The exact retention and pruning policy for historical recovery material.
- The exact packaging/deployment boundary if evidence shows a separate release owner is required.
- The exact accessibility parity target and assistive-technology depth if the evidence proves a narrower or broader release floor is needed.

## Program Stage 11 Fatal Question Inputs

Stage 11 is Fatal Question Review.
Stage 10 prepares the unresolved risks that Stage 11 must review, but it does not answer them here.

Unresolved risk inputs include:

- Can current work be lost silently?
- Can recovery report success without verification?
- Can interrupted restore leave ambiguous state?
- Can migration corrupt or silently discard truth?
- Can retention remove the only recoverable copy?
- Can operational evidence overclaim what was tested?

## Stage 12 Dependencies

- Restored-copy identity remains deferred to Stage 12.
- Any later true architecture or ownership question that emerges from operational-readiness evidence may also require Stage 12.
- Stage 12 is not the default home for ordinary operational questions that Stage 10 can answer through evidence.

## Dossier-Correction Rule

No dossier correction is required unless Stage 10 finds a proven contradiction or a missing owner boundary that cannot be resolved by evidence classification alone.
Presentation gaps, naming polish, or implementation details are not sufficient by themselves.

## Stage 10 Closure Criteria

Stage 10 may close only when:

- all four batches are complete,
- each obligation is classified as evidence, resolved doctrine, missing proof, genuine decision, Stage 11 input, Stage 12 dependency, or later implementation proof,
- no claim overstates what was directly observed,
- no silent retry, substitution, transmission, or truth mutation entered through the operational lane,
- no new connector or implementation authority was introduced,
- Stage 11 inputs are prepared,
- Stage 12 dependencies are explicit,
- and implementation remains blocked.
