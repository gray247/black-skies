# Stage 19 V1.0 Authority Alignment Decision

## 1. Decision

Jason explicitly decides that Stage 19 is the governed implementation and
acceptance program intended to culminate in Black Skies V1.0.

Stage 19 remains the implementation sequencing authority. Black Skies V1.0 is
the product milestone reached through Stage 19; it is not a separate or
competing stage system.

## 2. Prior-authority reconciliation

The Stage 17 plan, Stage 18 closure, Stage 19 entry, and the committed Stage 19
continuation-selection record authorized progressively bounded work. The
repository therefore previously described Stage 19 too narrowly to support a
whole-V1.0 claim. `stage19_v1_completion_program_reconciliation.md` preserves
that historical finding.

This author decision supplies the missing forward authority. It does not
retroactively turn bounded implementation evidence into manual acceptance or
release evidence.

## 3. Initial bounded sequence disposition

Packages `19.1` through `19.5` form the `Foundation Spine` package group and
close only the initial bounded local project-open/save/re-entry sequence, subject to final Package
`19.5` acceptance. They do not close Stage 19. They do not prove or complete
Black Skies V1.0.

Package `19.5` establishes focused automated verification of the integrated
spine. It does not claim manual acceptance, release readiness, packaging
completion, or entire-stage closure.

## 4. Continuing Stage 19 requirements

Stage 19 must continue through:

1. an approved V1.0 scope lock;
2. an approved master implementation and acceptance plan;
3. separately bounded and authorized remaining implementation packages;
4. manual happy-path, failure-path, and real writing-session acceptance;
5. stabilization and an accessibility floor;
6. packaging and install verification;
7. release-candidate verification;
8. release notes, known limitations, and final Stage 19/V1.0 closure.

Stage 19 closure requires the implementation, manual acceptance, and
release-readiness evidence selected by the approved master plan. No later
runtime mutation is authorized merely by this decision; each implementation
package remains separately bounded and authorized.

## 5. Governance boundaries

Protected evidence remains governed by its existing controls and is not
authorized for inspection or mutation by this decision. Previously excluded
systems are not automatically admitted into V1.0 or Stage 19. The V1.0 scope
lock must decide required and excluded capability boundaries without silently
expanding broad AI, critique, rewrite, connectors, restore/import, advanced
diagnostics, provenance/sync, or other deferred systems.

The charter in `stage19_v1_scope_lock_and_master_plan_charter.md` has now been
fulfilled by the V1.0 scope lock, master implementation/acceptance plan, and
initial salvage traceability matrix. Package `19.7` is the only selected next
package; it authorizes read-only/docs-only inventory and baseline work, not
runtime mutation.
