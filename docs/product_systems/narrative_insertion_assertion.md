## 2. Purpose

Define the smallest narrative foundation used by Black Skies so the product can reason about narrative structure without forcing prose, scenes, or Story Units to act as the root semantic primitive.

## 3. User Problem Solved

The writer needs a narrative unit small enough to support continuity, contradiction, relationships, sequencing, and later structural tooling without requiring immediate prose rewrite or forcing the writer into a rigid planning ritual before writing begins.

## 4. What The System Does

`Narrative Insertion / Assertion` provides:

- the smallest narrative identity unit,
- a durable narrative reference that can exist before or after prose,
- a narrative fact, event, implication, or claim that can be linked, contradicted, reordered, or grouped,
- a basis for continuity, critique, relationship mapping, gap modeling, memory, and later projection into prose or containers.

It allows Black Skies to retain narrative meaning even when the visible prose or containers are rearranged.

## 5. What The System Does Not Do

`Narrative Insertion / Assertion` does not:

- require prose to exist first,
- require scenes to exist first,
- require Story Units to exist first,
- require the user to enter ontology data before writing,
- claim that inferred or AI-produced structure is authored truth,
- resolve contradictions automatically,
- replace prose as the visible act of writing,
- replace outline, Story Unit, critique note, or projection view with one flat concept.

## 6. User-Facing Behavior

User-facing behavior may later include:

- direct insertion or assertion capture,

User-facing behavior must stay optional when it risks slowing direct writing.

## 16. Relationship To Narrative Insertion / Assertion

This dossier defines the foundation itself.

`Narrative Insertion / Assertion` is the smallest narrative unit because it can exist independently of:

- prose wording,
- scene or container placement,
- Story Unit grouping,
- outline hierarchy,
- critique display.

It is the base identity layer that later systems should consume rather than replace.

## 17. Relationship To Story Units

Story Units are optional higher-order groupings.
They may collect, stage, or organize insertions or assertions, but they are not required in order for insertions or assertions to exist.

Making Story Units mandatory would turn a higher-order organizational concept into an unwanted entry gate.

## 18. Relationship To Prose / Scene Projection

`Narrative Insertion / Assertion` feeds projection.
Projection does not define the insertion or assertion.

Prose and scene may express or contain narrative insertions or assertions, but they do not own the underlying narrative truth or identity by default.

## 19. Relationship To Writing Surface

The Writing Surface may:

- originate insertions or assertions through direct writing,
- later expose optional insertion or assertion context,
- continue writing without forcing explicit insertion or assertion manipulation.

The foundation must support writing rather than obstruct it.

## 20. Relationship To Command Center Surface

The Command Center is the likely place for deeper inspection, ordering, linking, contradiction, provenance, and gap views related to insertions or assertions.

That does not make the Command Center the authorial source of truth.

## 21. GUI Placement Principles

Placement rules:

- advanced structure views belong in contextual or summonable surfaces,
- ids and lineage should stay hidden unless needed,
- contradiction and relationship views should be inspectable without overwhelming normal writing.

## 34. Acceptance Criteria

This dossier is acceptable only if it states explicitly that:

- `Narrative Insertion / Narrative Assertion` is the smallest narrative unit,
- it differs from prose, scene, outline node, Story Unit, and critique note,
- it can exist before prose and also be discovered later,
- it supports reorder, contradiction, continuity, critique, relationship mapping, gaps, memory, and projection,
- durable identity is preserved,
- authored and inferred boundaries remain explicit,
- Story Units are not made mandatory,
- AI or inferred output does not become fake authored truth.

## Examples

Consider these insertions or assertions as separate durable units:

- `Larry got on a boat.`
- `Larry caught a fish.`
- `Larry had dinner with friends.`
- `Larry died of poison.`
- `Larry's wife poisoned him.`

These units can be:

- reordered,
- grouped differently,
- linked causally,
- contradicted by new material,
- attached to different projections later,
- re-expressed in different prose without losing identity.

Example relationship possibilities:

- `Larry's wife poisoned him.` may support or cause `Larry died of poison.`
- `Larry got on a boat.` may precede `Larry caught a fish.`
- `Larry had dinner with friends.` may occur before or after the fish event depending on later author choice.

The writer can change the presentation order or rewrite the prose around these units without immediately rewriting every structural relationship.
That is the point of using durable insertions or assertions as the foundation instead of treating visible prose order or container order as the only narrative truth.
