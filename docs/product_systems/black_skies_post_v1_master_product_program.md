## 5. Frozen Product Constitution

The following principles govern every later program unless Jason explicitly
changes them.

### 5.1 The Writer Owns The Page

- The author owns every final word.
- AI and analyzer output remains visibly advisory until the author acts.
- No suggestion, critique, signal, memory, outline proposal, projection,
  import, or generated text silently changes accepted prose or truth.
- Proposed and accepted material must remain visually distinguishable.

### 5.2 Writing Surface And Living Outline Are The Structural Heart

`Living Outline` is the accepted writer-facing name for the existing Outline
system working with the Writing Surface. It does not create a new truth owner.

The loop is bidirectional:

- selecting an outline item locates and highlights its related writing,
- selecting writing locates and highlights its structural context,
- planning may begin as fragments, pins, blobs, gaps, groups, beats, scenes,
  chapters, or larger spans,
- structure may emerge before, during, or after prose,
- outline movement may create a proposal or preview,
- accepted manuscript movement requires an explicit author-owned apply path,
- writing never requires outline setup first.

### 5.3 Command Center Supports; It Does Not Gate

- Writing remains available when support systems are closed, unavailable, or
  failing.
- The Command Center hosts deeper review, comparison, history, maps, queues,
  and results.
- It may aggregate information but owns none of the aggregated truth.

### 5.4 Companion Is A Small Door Into The System

The default Companion interaction is a small text bar at the top or bottom of
the active surface. Bottom placement is the starting recommendation because
it interferes less with document identity and navigation.

The author may type a natural request. The result opens in the Command
Center's main viewing area with visible source scope, owner, limitations, and
available actions.

Companion may explain, locate, compare, suggest, and route. It may not silently
write, canonize, remember, spend, transmit, export, delete, or change truth.

### 5.5 Signals Are Maturity-Aware And Author-Controlled

The starting signal-delivery choices are:

- `Off`: do not watch or surface that signal family,
- `Ask Only`: evaluate only when the author requests it,
- `Quiet`: watch without interruption and show a small indicator,
- `Alert`: interrupt only when the configured condition is met.

The starting project postures are:

- `Explore`: tolerate gaps and incomplete structure,
- `Develop`: surface repeated or increasingly consequential concerns quietly,
- `Finish`: increase review coverage and unresolved-issue visibility.

Word count is evidence, not law. A one-hundred-word outline should not be
scolded for natural gaps. A direct author request such as `stress-test this
premise` is available at any maturity. Proactive warnings should consider:

- project posture,
- manuscript and outline maturity,
- author-selected signal mode,
- confidence and evidence quality,
- recurrence,
- consequence if ignored,
- whether the author asked for early advice.

### 5.6 Functional UI Is V2; Unified Polish Is V3

V2 must build enough interface to display, understand, and act safely on each
backend result. Backend proof without a usable author workflow is incomplete.

V3 owns the major unified layout, interaction-language, comparison, and visual
polish program after at least two V2 workflows produce real author evidence.

### 5.7 Single-Screen First, Two-Screen Excellent

- Every important workflow must work well on one screen.
- A second monitor may provide a dedicated Command Center or alternate work
  surface.
- Two monitors are an enhancement, never a requirement.

### 5.8 Engines Are Replaceable

- No provider or local model becomes the product architecture.
- Local, paid, deterministic, and manual paths stay distinguishable.
- Heavy AI is introduced task by task, with preview, approval, cost, privacy,
  provenance, and destination acceptance kept separate.

### Program 4 - Companion And Owner Routing

Goal: make the minimal Companion bar a useful front door without turning it
into a universal owner.

First bounded workflow:

1. type a request,
2. show the active context scope,
3. route the request to the correct owning system,
4. show the result in the Command Center viewing surface,
5. allow only owner-safe actions such as copy, save advisory note, create an
   outline candidate, explain a signal, continue, or dismiss,
6. return focus to writing without changing the manuscript.

Conversation history remains temporary by default. Durable memory is not
introduced through this program.

### Program 5 - Long-Manuscript Intake And Structural Anchors

Goal: let an author bring in a long existing manuscript without manually
recreating every unit, while preserving the Writing Surface to Living Outline
loop as one ecosystem.

First bounded workflow:

1. accept a long, explicitly author-selected manuscript through a staged
   intake path,
2. preserve the original manuscript and its order unchanged,
3. derive stable source-linked structural anchors,
4. propose likely chapters or units only where evidence supports them,
5. represent uncertain divisions, gaps, and unplaced material as proposals
   rather than accepted structure,
6. allow the author to accept, rename, move, combine, split, or dismiss those
   proposals,
7. prove that reopening retains accepted anchors and does not silently promote
   rejected or inferred structure.

This program establishes the continuous-manuscript positions required by
later graphs and signals. It does not grant broad document-interchange,
Google Docs, bulk accepted-manuscript reorder, or automatic canon authority.

## 8. Efficient Delivery Rhythm

### 8.1 Long Automated Runs And Six Human Gates

Within and across adjacent low-risk workflow slices, the agent may run for
extended periods through:

1. contract and persistence batch,
2. owner bridge and process-boundary batch,
3. user-interface workflow batch,
4. end-to-end evidence batch,
5. automated repair and regression batches.

The exact number of automated batches may shrink or grow with risk. Human
review is consolidated into six gates:

1. `V2 combined review`: Critique Workbench plus Living Outline.
2. `V3 shell review`: contextual layout, Focus mode, and Companion bar.
3. `Long-manuscript review`: staged intake, proposed structure, accepted
   anchors, reopen behavior, and manuscript preservation.
4. `Intelligence and creation review`: signal posture, Emotion Graph V1, the
   first useful analyzers, and the first bounded creation or revision workflow.
5. `High-risk review`: paid or outbound AI, durable memory, background jobs,
   restore-over-current, destructive work, or truth-changing actions. Fresh
   approvals still apply where governing contracts require them.
6. `Final product review`: installed application and complete author journey
   before any later public milestone decision.

If a human gate fails, create a bounded repair batch, rerun automation, and
repeat the human gate only when the grouped workflow is whole again.

Human Gate 1 is complete as a learning gate. Its mechanical and safety
boundaries passed, and its required composition findings are routed to
Programs 3 and 4. Human Gate 2 is the next scheduled author validation.

Human validation is not required between these gates unless work encounters a
new product choice, owner conflict, destructive boundary, protected-content
decision, outbound transmission, paid spend, or other higher contract that
requires explicit author authority.

### 8.2 Commit And Push Rhythm

- The agent may prepare and verify one bounded batch.
- Jason reviews the concise evidence summary.
- Jason alone stages, commits, and pushes.
- A batch is not mixed with unrelated cleanup.
- A milestone closure receipt is used only for a real milestone or high-risk
  boundary, not every ordinary UI adjustment.

The agent should not interrupt Jason for ordinary implementation mechanics.
