  records rather than as a serial construction queue.

The plain-language version is:

> V1 built the house foundation. V2 proves that the important rooms work.
> V3 makes the whole house feel coherent, calm, and finished.

## 5. Frozen Product Constitution

The following principles govern every later program unless Jason explicitly
changes them.

### 5.1 The Writer Owns The Page

- The author owns every final word.
- AI and analyzer output remains visibly advisory until the author acts.
- No suggestion, critique, signal, memory, outline proposal, projection,
  import, or generated text silently changes accepted prose or truth.
- Proposed and accepted material must remain visually distinguishable.

### 5.2 Writing Surface And Living Outline Are The Structural Heart

`Living Outline` is the accepted writer-facing name for the existing Outline
system working with the Writing Surface. It does not create a new truth owner.

The loop is bidirectional:

- selecting an outline item locates and highlights its related writing,
- selecting writing locates and highlights its structural context,
- planning may begin as fragments, pins, blobs, gaps, groups, beats, scenes,
  chapters, or larger spans,
- structure may emerge before, during, or after prose,
- outline movement may create a proposal or preview,
- accepted manuscript movement requires an explicit author-owned apply path,
- writing never requires outline setup first.

### 5.3 Command Center Supports; It Does Not Gate

- Writing remains available when support systems are closed, unavailable, or
  failing.
- The Command Center hosts deeper review, comparison, history, maps, queues,
  and results.
- It may aggregate information but owns none of the aggregated truth.

### 5.4 Companion Is A Small Door Into The System

The default Companion interaction is a small text bar at the top or bottom of
the active surface. Bottom placement is the starting recommendation because
it interferes less with document identity and navigation.

The author may type a natural request. The result opens in the Command
Center's main viewing area with visible source scope, owner, limitations, and
available actions.

Companion may explain, locate, compare, suggest, and route. It may not silently
write, canonize, remember, spend, transmit, export, delete, or change truth.

### 5.5 Signals Are Maturity-Aware And Author-Controlled

The starting signal-delivery choices are:

- `Off`: do not watch or surface that signal family,
- `Ask Only`: evaluate only when the author requests it,
- `Quiet`: watch without interruption and show a small indicator,
- `Alert`: interrupt only when the configured condition is met.

The starting project postures are:

- `Explore`: tolerate gaps and incomplete structure,
- `Develop`: surface repeated or increasingly consequential concerns quietly,
- `Finish`: increase review coverage and unresolved-issue visibility.

Word count is evidence, not law. A one-hundred-word outline should not be
scolded for natural gaps. A direct author request such as `stress-test this
premise` is available at any maturity. Proactive warnings should consider:

- project posture,
- manuscript and outline maturity,
- author-selected signal mode,
- confidence and evidence quality,
- recurrence,
- consequence if ignored,
- whether the author asked for early advice.

### 5.6 Functional UI Is V2; Unified Polish Is V3

V2 must build enough interface to display, understand, and act safely on each
backend result. Backend proof without a usable author workflow is incomplete.

V3 owns the major unified layout, interaction-language, comparison, and visual
polish program after at least two V2 workflows produce real author evidence.

### 5.7 Single-Screen First, Two-Screen Excellent

- Every important workflow must work well on one screen.
- A second monitor may provide a dedicated Command Center or alternate work
  surface.
- Two monitors are an enhancement, never a requirement.

### 5.8 Engines Are Replaceable

- No provider or local model becomes the product architecture.
- Local, paid, deterministic, and manual paths stay distinguishable.
- Heavy AI is introduced task by task, with preview, approval, cost, privacy,
  provenance, and destination acceptance kept separate.

## 6. Product Sequence

This sequence contains eight programs, not forty-five serial dossier builds.
Each program may contain several automated batches, but it must end in a
complete author workflow.

### Program 1 - Complete Critique Workbench

Goal: finish the already-started selected-prose critique-to-advisory-note
workflow.

Remaining scope:

- run the end-to-end evidence batch,
- prove note survival across reopen and project isolation,
- prove prose cannot change through the workflow,
- prove cancellation, stale selection, provider failure, and copy or note-save
  failure remain honest,
- prepare Critique Workbench for the combined V2 human gate,
- create a repair batch only if automation or that combined review finds a
  problem.

Exit evidence:

- automated evidence is green,
- the workflow is ready for the combined V2 human gate with Living Outline,
- no broad critique-quality or provider-ranking claim is made.

### Program 2 - Prove The Living Outline Loop

Goal: make the Writing Surface and Living Outline operate as one useful,
bidirectional author workflow.

First bounded slice:

- create, label, and arrange lightweight outline material,
- link outline items to writing without requiring a scene-first model,
- click either side to locate and highlight the other,
- support fragments, gaps, and empty planning areas without warning noise,
- show authored, planned, inferred, and proposed structure distinctly,
- preview structural movement without silently reordering accepted prose,
- preserve identity and links across save and reopen,
- allow Focus mode to hide the outline instantly.

Deferred from this first slice:

- full graph visualization,
- automatic structure extraction,
- deep Story Unit lifecycle,
- accepted-manuscript bulk reorder,
- branching or what-if architecture,
- broad continuity and emotion overlays.

Exit evidence:

- the loop works on a real small project,
- direct writing remains possible with no outline,
- outline-first and prose-first paths both work,
- the combined V2 human gate reviews Critique Workbench and Living Outline in
  one author session.

### Program 3 - Contextual Product Shell

Goal: build the coherent V3 single-screen shell from proven workflow needs.

Starting layout:

- editor in the center,
- project navigation and Living Outline available beside it,
- only small relevant context visible by default,
- one-click Focus mode hides support panels,
- deeper review opens on demand,
- Command Center remains a separate supportive workspace,
- panels are resizable, collapsible, and remembered safely,
- the same product remains usable on one or two monitors.

This is where the visual concepts are reconciled into one product language.
It is not permission to build every dashboard card shown in a concept image.

### Program 4 - Companion And Owner Routing

Goal: make the minimal Companion bar a useful front door without turning it
into a universal owner.

First bounded workflow:

1. type a request,
2. show the active context scope,
3. route the request to the correct owning system,
4. show the result in the Command Center viewing surface,
5. allow only owner-safe actions such as copy, save advisory note, create an
   outline candidate, explain a signal, continue, or dismiss,
6. return focus to writing without changing the manuscript.

Conversation history remains temporary by default. Durable memory is not
introduced through this program.

### Program 5 - Signals And Story Intelligence

Goal: add useful lenses without creating an alarm dashboard.

Sequence:

1. implement the shared Off / Ask Only / Quiet / Alert posture,
2. implement Explore / Develop / Finish project posture,
3. build Emotion Graph V1 as the first visual story-intelligence lens,
4. prove continuity and one further high-value signal family end to end,
5. add further analyzers only when the shared interaction works,
6. keep deep maps and comparisons summonable in the Command Center.

Likely order after the shared signal contract:

- Emotion Graph V1,
- continuity,
- timeline and chronology,
- foreshadow and payoff,
- pacing and pressure,
- overused words, cliche, and senses analyzers,
- theme and relationship views,
- richer multi-character and intended-versus-observed emotion overlays.

Emotion Graph V1 begins after Human Gate 2 confirms the contextual V3 shell
and Companion placement. It depends on the Living Outline's stable story
positions so graph points can link back to real writing and structure.

Its first bounded slice includes:

- source-linked emotional movement across Living Outline positions,
- visibly distinct author-planned, manuscript-observed, and inferred lanes,
- optional character lanes using author labels or accepted character
  references where available,
- click-to-jump between a graph point, related writing, and outline context,
- summonable Command Center detail with only quiet contextual cues beside the
  Writing Surface,
- no AI dependency, automatic canonization, mandatory emotion taxonomy,
  psychiatric diagnosis, or universal emotional-success score.

The order may change from author evidence. No analyzer earns a permanent
dashboard tile merely because a dossier exists.

### Program 6 - Creation, Revision, And Story Development

No second provider, automatic routing, background execution, persistent AI
memory, or silent fallback is added merely to make the architecture look

## 8. Efficient Delivery Rhythm

### 8.1 Long Automated Runs And Five Human Gates

Within and across adjacent low-risk workflow slices, the agent may run for
extended periods through:

1. contract and persistence batch,
2. owner bridge and process-boundary batch,
3. user-interface workflow batch,
4. end-to-end evidence batch,
5. automated repair and regression batches.

The exact number of automated batches may shrink or grow with risk. Human
review is consolidated into five gates:

1. `V2 combined review`: Critique Workbench plus Living Outline.
2. `V3 shell review`: contextual layout, Focus mode, and Companion bar.
3. `Intelligence review`: signal posture, Emotion Graph V1, the first useful
   analyzers, and the first bounded creation or revision workflow.
4. `High-risk review`: paid or outbound AI, durable memory, background jobs,
   restore-over-current, destructive work, or truth-changing actions. Fresh
   approvals still apply where governing contracts require them.
5. `Final product review`: installed application and complete author journey
   before any later public milestone decision.

If a human gate fails, create a bounded repair batch, rerun automation, and
repeat the human gate only when the grouped workflow is whole again.

Human validation is not required between these gates unless work encounters a
new product choice, owner conflict, destructive boundary, protected-content
decision, outbound transmission, paid spend, or other higher contract that
requires explicit author authority.

### 8.2 Commit And Push Rhythm

- The agent may prepare and verify one bounded batch.
- Jason reviews the concise evidence summary.
- Jason alone stages, commits, and pushes.
- A batch is not mixed with unrelated cleanup.
- A milestone closure receipt is used only for a real milestone or high-risk
  boundary, not every ordinary UI adjustment.

### 8.3 Risk-Proportional Governance

| Tier | Example | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A - routine | styling, labels, local component behavior, non-owning view state | focused automation plus affected regression; normally no separate human check |
| B - bounded boundary | persistence sidecar, IPC, owner handoff, advisory conversion, project isolation | contract tests, integration tests, end-to-end workflow, one completed-slice human review |
| C - high risk | accepted truth mutation, destructive migration, restore-over-current, paid/outbound work, protected content, durable memory, provider switching | dossier decision, explicit authorization, adversarial and recovery evidence, full relevant qualification, explicit human acceptance |

### 8.4 Question Discipline

The agent should not interrupt Jason for ordinary implementation mechanics.

Questions are grouped at slice boundaries and asked only when the answer would
change:

- author workflow,
- truth ownership,
- privacy or protected-content behavior,
- money or outbound transmission,
- destructive or hard-to-reverse behavior,
- a product concept that the dossiers deliberately leave open.

## 9. Deferral Rules And Reopening Triggers

Every deferral needs a named trigger. `Later` by itself is invalid.

| Deferred family | Resolution point | Reopening trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dossier-completion wording and field detail | Planning batch for the first workflow that needs it | The slice cannot define a stable contract or understandable UI from current category-4 doctrine |
| Accepted-manuscript reorder | Later Living Outline slice | A proven preview-only workflow creates real author demand for committed reorder |
| Durable AI memory or preference learning | Memory-specific product decision before design | A proven workflow cannot deliver repeated value without retained derived state |
| Second provider or automatic routing | Heavy Intelligence program | Task-specific receipts prove an alternate route is useful and Jason approves the routing decision |
| Background or overnight jobs | Heavy Intelligence program | A proven task is too slow for foreground use and has safe revalidation, cancellation, retention, and budget rules |
| Paid or outbound expansion | Named task package | A local/manual workflow is proven and the outbound value justifies privacy, cost, and approval work |
| Third-party plugins | Post-first-party analyzer review | Repeated demand cannot be met through internal rubrics or first-party analyzers |
| Connectors | Existing Workflow Proof plus Missing Connector Review gate | A complete author workflow demonstrates that a connector is necessary rather than convenient |
| Branching / what-if architecture | Later structural review | Prototype arrangements cannot satisfy a real author workflow without durable branches |
| Research / Deep Research | Remains non-promoted | Jason explicitly reopens product scope and establishes a current owner and workflow |
| Voice / dictation | Remains excluded | Jason explicitly reopens product scope and defines accessibility and provenance boundaries |
| Repository cleanup and archive | Separate later cleanup milestone, before public release | The post-V1 product authority map is stable enough that historical material can be classified without losing needed evidence |

## 10. GUI Direction

The contextual writing view is the starting V3 default:

- center: Writing Surface,
- side: navigation and Living Outline,
- small context: only what helps the current passage or structural location,
- Focus: one click removes support UI,

The concept images are inspiration and interaction evidence. They are not
literal feature checklists. Dense dashboards, decorative metrics, universal

## 11. Program Completion Rules

A workflow program is complete only when:

- its owner and non-owner boundaries are explicit,
- its automated evidence is green,
- failure and degraded behavior are honest,
- direct writing remains available when safe,
- persistence and project isolation are proved when applicable,
- the UI makes advisory, candidate, accepted, stale, failed, and unavailable
  states understandable,
- one human review of the complete workflow passes,
- deferred items have exact later homes and reopening triggers,
- no broader capability claim is made than the evidence supports.
