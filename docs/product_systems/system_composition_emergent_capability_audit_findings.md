## Findings

### SC-01 — Cross-domain accepted-fact routing

- Status: Stage 3 regression candidate
- Problem: Relationship Map, Emotion Graph, timelines, cards, lore, and manuscript truth can describe related facts while retaining different owners. Edge-case accepted-fact routing needs sharper dossier wording.
- Ownership risk: A support system may appear to own accepted character, relationship, emotional, or chronological truth.
- First resolution stage: Stage 3 - Dossier Regression and Doctrine Propagation Audit
- Reopening trigger: An individual dossier lacks explicit routing from analysis or projection to the correct accepted-truth owner.
- Closure condition: All affected dossiers clearly distinguish analysis, planning, projection, and accepted truth.

### SC-02 — Memory and retrieval distinction

- Status: Stage 3 regression candidate
- Problem: Memory Lab and Project Index / Search / Retrieval can return related material but own different functions.
- Ownership risk: Retrieved source material may be mistaken for remembered context, or memory may be treated as evidence.
- First resolution stage: Stage 3 - Dossier Regression and Doctrine Propagation Audit
- Reopening trigger: Either dossier fails to preserve source identity, memory status, evidence quality, or deletion/forgetting boundaries.
- Closure condition: Retrieval and governed recall remain visibly distinct.

### SC-03 — Composed negative-state visibility

- Status: Stage 3 regression candidate
- Problem: Stale, conflicted, archived, deleted, forgotten, blocked, refused, detached, and recovery-first states exist across systems but may be exposed inconsistently.
- Ownership risk: Users may interpret unavailable, stale, or partial support material as current or accepted.
- First resolution stage: Stage 3 - Dossier Regression and Doctrine Propagation Audit
- Reopening trigger: An affected dossier lacks the negative states required by its composed workflows.
- Closure condition: Relevant dossiers expose domain-appropriate negative states without creating one universal lifecycle.

### SC-04 — Apparent authority across composed views

- Status: Stage 3 regression candidate and Stage 6 workflow-proof input
- Problem: Cards, maps, graphs, timelines, package previews, provenance overlays, restore previews, Companion explanations, and Command Center summaries can appear more authoritative than their actual status.
- Ownership risk: Presentation may silently collapse advisory, reviewed, projected, restored, and accepted states.
- First resolution stage: Stage 3 - Dossier Regression and Doctrine Propagation Audit
- Later proof stage: Stage 6 - Cross-System Workflow Proofs
- Reopening trigger: A dossier lacks adequate authority labeling, or a workflow prototype still implies acceptance before explicit owner action.
- Closure condition: Dossier doctrine is explicit and later workflows preserve visible authority distinctions.

### SC-05 — Recovery, arrangement, and current-truth coordination

- Status: Stage 6 workflow-proof candidate
- Problem: Restore, re-entry, arrangement, projection, and current manuscript state may disagree while remaining individually valid.
- Ownership risk: Recovery or arrangement infrastructure may silently become a structure or truth owner.
- First resolution stage: Stage 6 - Cross-System Workflow Proofs
- Reopening trigger: A restore, reorder, regroup, split, merge, or surface-reentry workflow cannot preserve current truth and non-authoritative arrangement state simultaneously.
- Closure condition: Workflow proof preserves existing owners and keeps recovery and arrangement states non-owning.

### SC-06 — Distributed AI path presentation

- Status: Stage 3 regression candidate and Stage 6 workflow-proof input
- Problem: Routing, budget, package construction, protection, provenance, queue state, and destination acceptance are distributed but may appear as one approval state.
- Ownership risk: A summary surface may become a hidden AI-clearance owner.
- First resolution stage: Stage 3 - Dossier Regression and Doctrine Propagation Audit
- Later proof stage: Stage 6 - Cross-System Workflow Proofs
- Reopening trigger: Dossier wording or a workflow presentation collapses separate AI authorities into one apparent clearance state.
- Closure condition: Distributed ownership remains explicit and workflows present a coherent path without creating a universal AI owner.
