## 9. Evidence Taxonomy

- Doctrine resolved means repository doctrine already settles the boundary.
- Workflow-boundary proof means doctrine-backed workflow-proof evidence without live runtime, harness, packaged-application, or test execution evidence.
- Existing operational evidence means bounded observed runtime, harness, packaged-application, or test execution evidence that directly verifies the claimed behavior.
- Historical operational evidence means observed execution records that are useful as historical evidence but are not current product authority.
- Missing operational evidence means required bounded runtime, harness, packaged-application, or test execution evidence that does not yet exist in the current record.
- Genuine author decision means the boundary needs a product choice, not more evidence.
- Program Stage 11 Fatal Question input means the boundary exposes unresolved risk that should be carried into Fatal Question Review.
- Stage 12 dependency means the boundary is really an architecture identity or ownership question that Stage 10 should not settle.
- Later implementation proof means the boundary is likely measurable later, but not yet operationally evidenced.
