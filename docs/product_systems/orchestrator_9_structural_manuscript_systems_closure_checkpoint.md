## Settled Structural Model

### Narrative Truth

- Narrative Insertion is the action or workflow for introducing narrative material.
- Narrative Assertion is the smallest durable manuscript-truth unit.
- Narrative Assertion owns accepted manuscript content, durable identity, and authoritative manuscript order.
- Direct author writing in the authoritative manuscript is non-gated and immediately accepted.
- Direct author reorder in authoritative manuscript context is immediately accepted.
- Candidate or proposed state is limited to intentional proposal, staging, prototype, imported-review, or AI-proposal contexts.

### Story Unit

- Story Unit is optional grouping and reference state.
- It does not own or contain manuscript truth.
- Multiple Story Units may reference the same Narrative Assertion.
- Story Units may exist empty.
- Archive preserves the unit and references in recoverable inactive state.
- Dissolve removes the grouping and detaches references without deleting assertions.
- Assertion deletion remains a separate Narrative Assertion operation.

### Outline

- Outline owns planning hierarchy, planning order, alternatives, and prototype arrangements.
- Planning order may differ from manuscript order.
- Prototype arrangements remain advisory until explicitly applied.
- Preview never mutates manuscript truth.
- Partial apply, stale proposal, dismissal, expiration, and abandonment are explicit lifecycle states.
- Accepted proposal application routes through the appropriate owner.

### Projection and Containers

- Prose / Scene Projection is rendering, grouping, navigation, and compatibility.
- Scene and chapter containers may retain durable identity.
- Container identity does not make scene or chapter the manuscript foundation.
- Assertions remain valid without scene or chapter assignment.
- Projection output is not a second truth store.
- Authoritative edit-through routes to Narrative Assertion and is immediately accepted.
- Stale or unavailable source anchors are exposed rather than silently reconstructed.

### Visual Arrangement View

- Visual Arrangement View is a non-owning display and action-request layer.
- Saved card position, lanes, filters, zoom, and workspace arrangement remain noncanonical visual state.
- Card position alone does not establish manuscript order.
- Structural proposals require preview and owner-routed application.
- Stale references remain visible as stale or unavailable.
- Surface-local navigation and explicit handoffs remain preserved.
- Corkboard remains legacy or informal terminology, not a separate system or durable owner.

### Protection and Recovery

- Protected, excluded, hidden, local-only, never-send, masked, and approved-summary boundaries remain enforced.
- Projection and visual convenience cannot leak protected content.
- Source orientation and return posture do not prove save integrity.
- Snapshots and recovery systems retain restoration authority.
