## Surface Architecture Conclusions

- Writing Surface remains sovereign and quiet by default.
- Command Center remains non-sovereign and is not a universal inbox.
- Companion remains optional, contextual, and non-owning.
- Direct writing remains complete without AI.
- Projections do not inherit ownership.

## Advisory-Review Conclusions

- Advisory output is not acceptance.
- Notes, Signals, critique findings, continuity conflicts, rewrite candidates, and partial acceptance remain distinct.
- Candidate visibility is not owner acceptance.
- Reorder preview is not applied order.
- Return to Writing Surface remains explicit and safe.

## Persistence, Recovery, And Interchange Conclusions

- Save, snapshot, backup, archive, export, import, restore, inspection, and verification remain distinct.
- Restore-as-copy remains distinct from restore-as-current.
- Restore-as-current remains destructive and approval-gated.
- Import does not automatically become manuscript truth.
- Warning burden stays proportional and safe-next-action oriented.

## Navigation, Focus, Keyboard, Accessibility, And Multi-Screen Conclusions

- Navigation remains distinct from workflow ownership.
- Focus remains predictable and deliberate.
- Keyboard completion remains possible for critical review and recovery paths.
- Dismissal remains distinct from resolution.
- Single-screen use remains complete.
- Multi-screen use remains optional.
- Large-font behavior must not hide critical state or actions.

## Doctrine-Resolved Non-Decisions
