## 2. Truth Ownership

- `Author Intent / Story Setup` owns accepted project-level creative
  intent and project truth.
- `Author Intent / Story Setup` is not a universal fact owner.
- `Character Cards` own accepted structured character facts.
- `Lore Cards` own accepted structured lore and world facts.
- `Narrative Insertion / Narrative Assertion` retains accepted
  manuscript content and authoritative manuscript order.
- Direct manuscript writing remains non-gated.
- Candidate, inferred, detected, and advisory state never becomes
  accepted truth automatically.

## 3. Cross-Domain Ownership

- Every accepted cross-domain fact has one primary truth owner.
- Other systems hold secondary references rather than duplicate
  authoritative records.
- Owner transfer is explicit and provenance-bearing.
- Mixed-domain claims route according to what the claim principally
  asserts.
- `Relationship Map` never becomes an accepted-fact owner.

## 4. Relationship, Emotion, And Theme Support

- `Relationship Map` stores representations, secondary references,
  advisory inferences, and display state.
- `Emotion Graph` separates accepted source references, planned
  development, detected movement, and intended reader effect.
- Accepted character emotional facts route to `Character Cards`.
- Accepted project-level emotional or reader-effect intent routes to
  `Author Intent / Story Setup`.
- `Theme System` separates author-owned Story Theme intent, manuscript
  evidence, advisory detection and interpretation, and display or trend
  state.
- Detected Story Themes remain advisory.
- Story Theme remains distinct from UI appearance or visual theme.

## 5. Timeline, Pacing, And Pressure

- `Timeline` does not become a universal event-fact owner.
- Story chronology remains distinct from manuscript order, `Outline`
  planning order, projected organization, and reader revelation order.
- `Timeline / Pacing / Pressure` remains one dossier with three
  explicit internal modules.
- Pacing and Pressure remain advisory analysis.
- Pressure remains distinct from pacing and emotional truth.
- Bounded history remains labeled, on-demand, and non-prominent.

## 6. Foreshadow And Payoff

- `Foreshadow / Payoff` tracks planned and detected setup-to-resolution
  relationships.
- It does not own source facts or manuscript truth.
- Planned structural placement routes to `Outline`.
- Creative promises or intent route to `Author Intent / Story Setup`.
- Manuscript evidence remains owned by
  `Narrative Insertion / Narrative Assertion`.
- Character and lore facts remain owned by their card systems.
- Detected links remain advisory.
- Unresolved or intentionally unresolved links remain quiet by default
  but summonable.

## 7. Review And Protection Posture

- Findings do not automatically create Notes or Signals.
- Review collections remain non-owning.
- Recurrence creates linked advisory candidates rather than
  automatically reopening durable work.
- Protected, hidden, excluded, local-only, never-send, masked,
  author-known, character-known, and reader-visible boundaries remain
  enforced.
- Cards, maps, graphs, previews, exports, Companion answers, Memory Lab
  recall, and AI packages must not leak restricted information.
